Focus: Metabolic Flexibility

This week's meals are built around Metabolic Flexibility. Each plate is designed to be whole-food, home-cook-friendly, and easy to prepare in 40 minutes or less.

Ingredients
  • 6 eggs
  • 3 oz smoked wild salmon
  • 1 avocado
  • 2 Tbsp fresh dill
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1/4 tsp sea salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 375 F and oil a 6-cup muffin tin.
  • Whisk eggs with salt and pepper, then fold in torn salmon and dill.
  • Divide into the tin and bake 15 to 18 minutes until set.
  • Top warm cups with sliced avocado and serve.

Ingredients
  • 3 Tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 cup full-fat coconut milk
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 cup sliced almonds
  • 1/2 cup raspberries
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Stir chia, coconut milk, vanilla, and cinnamon in a jar.
  • Chill at least 20 minutes until thickened.
  • Toast almonds in a dry skillet 3 minutes until golden.
  • Top pudding with raspberries and toasted almonds.

Ingredients
  • 8 oz grass-fed ground beef
  • 1 medium sweet potato, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, diced
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/4 tsp sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Saute sweet potato in olive oil over medium heat 10 minutes until tender.
  • Add onion and cook 3 minutes.
  • Push aside, brown the beef with paprika and salt 6 minutes.
  • Stir in spinach until wilted and serve hot.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup almond flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 small zucchini, grated
  • 1/4 cup coconut milk
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 2 Tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup walnuts, chopped
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Squeeze water from grated zucchini with a towel.
  • Whisk almond flour, eggs, coconut milk, and baking soda.
  • Fold in zucchini and walnuts.
  • Cook scoops in buttered skillet 3 minutes per side until golden.

Ingredients
  • 6 eggs
  • 6 oz ground turkey
  • 2 cups chopped kale
  • 1/4 cup crumbled goat cheese
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp fennel seed
  • 1/4 tsp sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 375 F.
  • Brown turkey with fennel and salt in an oven-safe skillet 6 minutes.
  • Add kale and cook until wilted.
  • Pour whisked eggs over, top with goat cheese, and bake 15 minutes until set.

Ingredients
  • 3/4 cup cooked quinoa
  • 1/2 cup coconut milk
  • 2 Tbsp pistachios
  • 1/2 cup blueberries
  • 1 Tbsp almond butter
  • 1/2 tsp cardamom
  • 1 tsp honey
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Warm quinoa with coconut milk and cardamom over low heat 4 minutes.
  • Stir in almond butter until creamy.
  • Spoon into a bowl.
  • Top with blueberries, pistachios, and a drizzle of honey.

Ingredients
  • 2 avocados, halved and pitted
  • 4 eggs
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 2 Tbsp fresh chives
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1/4 tsp sea salt
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 400 F and scoop out a bit of each avocado half.
  • Set avocados in a baking dish and crack an egg into each.
  • Season with salt and red pepper, then bake 15 minutes.
  • Top with tomatoes and chives, drizzle with olive oil.

Ingredients
  • 10 oz ahi tuna steak
  • 4 cups mixed greens
  • 2 soft-boiled eggs
  • 1/2 cup green beans
  • 1/4 cup kalamata olives
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/4 tsp sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Sear tuna in a hot oiled skillet 90 seconds per side, then slice.
  • Blanch green beans 3 minutes and cool.
  • Arrange greens, beans, halved eggs, and olives on a plate.
  • Top with tuna and whisk olive oil, lemon, and salt into a dressing.

Ingredients
  • 12 oz cooked shredded chicken

  • 1 head butter lettuce
  • 1/4 cup diced red onion

  • 1/4 cup cilantro

Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Mix chicken with mashed avocado, lime, cumin, and onion.

Ingredients
  • 12 oz shrimp, peeled
  • 1 cup cooked cannellini beans
  • 2 cups arugula
  • 1/2 cup roasted red pepper, sliced
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1/4 tsp sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toss shrimp with garlic, 1 Tbsp oil, and salt.
  • Grill or sear shrimp 2 minutes per side.
  • Combine beans, arugula, and red pepper in a bowl.

Ingredients
  • 10 oz roasted turkey breast, diced
  • 4 cups romaine, chopped
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs
  • 1/4 cup crumbled blue cheese
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 3 slices cooked bacon
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp apple cider vinegar

  • Lay romaine on a platter.

Ingredients
  • 8 oz cooked wild salmon, flaked
  • 8 rice paper wrappers
  • 1 cucumber, julienned
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 cup shredded carrot
  • 1/4 cup mint leaves
  • 3 Tbsp coconut aminos
  • 1 Tbsp sesame oil
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Dip a rice paper wrapper in warm water 10 seconds to soften.
  • Layer salmon, cucumber, avocado, carrot, and mint near one edge.
  • Fold sides in and roll tightly.
  • Repeat and serve with coconut aminos whisked with sesame oil.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups spinach

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 cup feta, crumbled
  • 1/4 cup red onion, diced
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil

  • Combine chickpeas, cucumber, tomatoes, and onion in a bowl.

Ingredients
  • 4 wild salmon fillets
  • 1 head cauliflower, chopped
  • 3 Tbsp butter
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp fresh thyme
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 400 F and roast salmon topped with thyme and lemon 15 minutes.
  • Steam cauliflower 12 minutes until soft.
  • Blend cauliflower with butter, salt, and pepper until smooth.
  • Serve salmon over the mash with a drizzle of olive oil.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb grass-fed ground beef
  • 4 zucchini, halved lengthwise
  • 1 cup marinara
  • 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp Italian herbs
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 400 F and scoop out zucchini centers.
  • Brown beef with garlic and herbs 7 minutes, then stir in marinara.
  • Fill zucchini with beef and top with mozzarella.
  • Bake 20 minutes until bubbly and tender.

Ingredients
  • 6 bone-in chicken thighs
  • 1 lb asparagus, trimmed
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 425 F.
  • Toss chicken with olive oil, lemon, garlic, oregano, and salt.
  • Roast chicken skin-up on a sheet pan 25 minutes.
  • Add asparagus for the final 10 minutes until crisp-tender.

Ingredients
  • 4 cod fillets
  • 1/2 cup kalamata olives, chopped
  • 2 Tbsp capers
  • 3 cups broccolini
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 Tbsp parsley
  • 1/4 tsp sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Pat cod dry and sear in oiled skillet 4 minutes per side.
  • Mix olives, capers, parsley, lemon, and 1 Tbsp oil for tapenade.
  • Saute broccolini in remaining oil 6 minutes.
  • Spoon tapenade over cod and serve with broccolini.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb ground turkey
  • 1 spaghetti squash
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup almond flour
  • 1 cup marinara
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp Italian herbs
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 400 F and roast halved squash cut-side down 30 minutes.
  • Mix turkey, egg, almond flour, herbs, and salt, then form meatballs.
  • Brown meatballs in oiled skillet, add marinara, and simmer 10 minutes.
  • Scrape squash into strands and top with meatballs and sauce.
