Focus: Blood Sugar Balance

This week's meals are built around Blood Sugar Balance. Each plate is designed to be whole-food, home-cook-friendly, and easy to prepare in 40 minutes or less.

Ingredients
  • 3 pasture-raised eggs
  • 1/2 cup diced zucchini
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1 cup baby spinach
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1/4 avocado
  • Sea salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Saute zucchini and tomatoes in olive oil 4 minutes.
  • Add spinach and wilt.
  • Pour in whisked eggs and scramble until set.
  • Top with avocado, salt, and pepper.
