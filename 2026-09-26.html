Focus: Hormone Harmony

This week's meals are built around Hormone Harmony. Each plate is designed to be whole-food, home-cook-friendly, and easy to prepare in 40 minutes or less.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 cups whole-milk Greek yogurt
  • 2 Tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 2 Tbsp raw pumpkin seeds, chopped
  • 1/2 cup blackberries
  • 1 Tbsp almond butter
  • 1 tsp raw honey
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Grind the flaxseed fresh if you can, since the oils go rancid quickly.
  • Spoon the yogurt into two bowls and swirl in the almond butter.
  • Scatter the flax and pumpkin seeds evenly over the top.
  • Add the blackberries, honey, and cinnamon.

Ingredients
  • 8 eggs
  • 2 cups broccoli florets, finely chopped
  • 4 oz goat cheese, crumbled
  • 1 cup broccoli sprouts
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 375F.
  • Cook the broccoli and garlic in olive oil in an oven-safe skillet for 5 minutes.
  • Whisk the eggs with cream, salt, and pepper and pour over the broccoli.
  • Dot with goat cheese and bake 18 minutes until just set in the center.
  • Pile the raw broccoli sprouts on top after it comes out of the oven.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 cups unsweetened coconut milk
  • 1/2 avocado
  • 2 Tbsp raw cacao powder
  • 1 tsp maca powder
  • 2 Tbsp hemp hearts
  • 1 Tbsp almond butter
  • 1 pitted date
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Pour the coconut milk into the blender first.
  • Add the avocado, cacao, maca, hemp hearts, almond butter, date, and cinnamon.
  • Blend on high for a full minute until completely smooth and thick.
  • Taste and add a second date only if it needs it.

Ingredients
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 beets, roasted and sliced
  • 2 cups arugula
  • 1/2 cup sauerkraut
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Roast the beets at 400F for 45 minutes, then peel and slice.
  • Poach or soft-boil the eggs to your liking.
  • Dress the arugula with olive oil and lemon juice.
  • Arrange the greens, beets, sauerkraut, and avocado on two plates.
  • Set the eggs on top and finish with caraway seeds and cracked pepper.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup quinoa, rinsed well
  • 1 can full-fat coconut milk
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 cup dried apricots, halved
  • 1/3 cup sesame seeds, toasted
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp cardamom
  • Pinch of sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Simmer the quinoa with coconut milk, water, vanilla, and salt for 18 minutes.
  • Meanwhile simmer the apricots with a splash of water and the cardamom for 8 minutes.
  • Stir the porridge until creamy and loosen with water if needed.
  • Top each bowl with apricots and toasted sesame seeds.

Ingredients
  • 4 collard leaves, stems shaved flat
  • 8 oz smoked salmon
  • 2 cups shredded red cabbage
  • 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
  • 2 Tbsp whole-grain mustard
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 Tbsp fresh dill
  • 1 avocado, sliced
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Blanch the collard leaves for 30 seconds and shock them in ice water so they roll without cracking.
  • Stir the yogurt with mustard, lemon juice, and dill, then toss with the cabbage.
  • Lay a collard leaf flat and add slaw, salmon, and avocado near one end.
  • Fold in the sides and roll tightly, then halve on the diagonal.

Ingredients
  • 2 large sweet potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 4 links chicken apple sausage, sliced
  • 6 eggs
  • 2 cups chopped kale
  • 1/2 cup coconut milk
  • 2 Tbsp coconut oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp fresh sage, minced
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 375F and grease a 9x13 baking dish.
  • Layer the sweet potato slices across the bottom and brush with melted coconut oil.
  • Bake 20 minutes until the potatoes start to soften.
  • Scatter the sausage and kale over the top.
  • Whisk the eggs with coconut milk, paprika, and sage, pour over, and bake 25 minutes more.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb Brussels sprouts, shaved thin
  • 1 crisp apple, julienned
  • 2/3 cup walnuts, toasted
  • 1/3 cup shaved Pecorino
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 3 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 Tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp maple syrup
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Shave the Brussels sprouts as thin as possible with a mandoline or sharp knife.
  • Whisk the olive oil, vinegar, Dijon, and maple syrup into a dressing.
  • Toss the sprouts with the dressing and let them sit 15 minutes to soften.
  • Fold in the apple and walnuts.
  • Top with shaved Pecorino just before serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 head cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 1 can chickpeas, rinsed and dried
  • 1 1/2 cups cooked millet
  • 1/3 cup tahini
  • 1 cup parsley and cilantro, packed
  • 3 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 1 tsp cumin
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Roast the cauliflower and chickpeas at 425F with olive oil and cumin for 30 minutes.
  • Blend the tahini, herbs, lemon juice, garlic, and enough cold water to make a pourable green sauce.
  • Divide the millet among bowls.
  • Pile on the roasted cauliflower and chickpeas.
  • Pour the herb tahini over everything.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb cooked turkey breast, sliced
  • 1 large bunch lacinato kale, ribs removed
  • 1/3 cup sunflower seeds, toasted
  • 1/3 cup Greek yogurt
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 anchovy fillets, minced
  • 2 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 clove garlic, grated
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Slice the kale into ribbons and massage with a little olive oil and salt for 2 minutes.
  • Whisk the yogurt, anchovy, lemon juice, garlic, and remaining olive oil into a dressing.
  • Toss the kale with the dressing and let it sit 10 minutes.
  • Add the turkey and Parmesan.
  • Finish with toasted sunflower seeds.

Ingredients
  • 2 sweet potatoes, cubed
  • 1 1/2 cups cooked black rice
  • 8 oz baked tofu, cubed
  • 2 cups shredded napa cabbage
  • 3 Tbsp white miso paste
  • 2 Tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 Tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 Tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 Tbsp sesame seeds
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Roast the sweet potato at 425F for 28 minutes until the edges caramelize.
  • Whisk the miso, rice vinegar, sesame oil, ginger, and a splash of water into a dressing.
  • Toss the napa cabbage with half the dressing.
  • Build bowls with black rice, sweet potato, tofu, and cabbage.
  • Spoon the rest of the dressing over and add sesame seeds.

Ingredients
  • 2 tins sardines in olive oil, drained
  • 1 fennel bulb, shaved thin
  • 2 oranges, segmented
  • 1/2 cup Castelvetrano olives, torn
  • 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1/4 cup parsley leaves
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Soak the shaved fennel and onion in ice water 10 minutes, then drain well.
  • Segment the oranges over a bowl so you keep the juice.
  • Whisk the reserved juice with olive oil and vinegar.
  • Toss the fennel, onion, oranges, and olives with the dressing.
  • Lay the sardines on top and scatter with parsley.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 cups cooked black lentils
  • 3 cups shredded red cabbage
  • 3/4 cup crumbled feta
  • 1/2 cup fresh mint, torn
  • 1/3 cup pistachios, chopped
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 3 Tbsp pomegranate molasses
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk the olive oil, pomegranate molasses, and lemon juice together.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb chicken thighs, sliced
  • 6 cups chicken bone broth
  • 6 oz shiitake mushrooms, sliced
  • 4 heads baby bok choy, halved
  • 2 Tbsp grated ginger
  • 4 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 2 Tbsp tamari
  • 1 Tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 3 scallions, sliced
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Simmer the broth with ginger, garlic, and shiitakes for 10 minutes.
  • Add the chicken and poach gently for 8 minutes until cooked through.
  • Add the bok choy and cook 3 minutes until the stems are just tender.
  • Season with tamari and sesame oil.
  • Ladle into bowls and top with scallions.

Ingredients
  • 4 wild salmon fillets (6 oz each)
  • 1 1/2 lb Brussels sprouts, halved
  • 1/2 cup pomegranate seeds
  • 1/3 cup hazelnuts, toasted and chopped
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 1 Tbsp honey
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Roast the Brussels sprouts cut-side down at 425F with olive oil for 25 minutes.
  • Season the salmon and add it to the pan for the last 12 minutes.
  • Warm the balsamic and honey in a small pan until slightly syrupy.
  • Plate the salmon over the sprouts.
  • Drizzle with the glaze and top with pomegranate and hazelnuts.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 lb grass-fed sirloin, sliced thin
  • 4 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 can full-fat coconut milk
  • 3 Tbsp red curry paste
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 Tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 Tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 2 Tbsp coconut oil
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Sear the beef in coconut oil over high heat for 90 seconds, then set aside.
  • Fry the curry paste and ginger in the same pan for 1 minute.
  • Add the coconut milk and simmer 5 minutes to thicken.
  • Add the broccoli and bell pepper and cook 5 minutes.
  • Return the beef, then finish with fish sauce and lime juice.

Ingredients
  • 8 bone-in chicken thighs
  • 1 1/2 lb parsnips, cut into batons
  • 4 cups watercress
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped
  • 1 head garlic, halved crosswise
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 425F.
  • Toss the parsnips and halved garlic head with olive oil on a sheet pan.
  • Season the chicken heavily with salt, pepper, and rosemary and set it skin-side up on the pan.
  • Roast 40 minutes until the skin crisps and the parsnips caramelize.
  • Squeeze the softened garlic over everything and serve on a bed of watercress with lemon juice.

Ingredients
  • 4 cod fillets (6 oz each)
  • 2 leeks, sliced into thin rounds
  • 1 fennel bulb, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup pitted green olives
  • 1 lemon, thinly sliced
  • 4 Tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup dry white wine
  • 2 Tbsp fresh thyme
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 400F and cut four large parchment squares.
  • Divide the leeks and fennel among the squares and set a cod fillet on each.
  • Add olives, lemon slices, a Tbsp of butter, a splash of wine, and thyme to each packet.
  • Fold the parchment into sealed parcels, crimping the edges tightly.
  • Bake 16 minutes and open at the table so the steam is part of it.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 lb lamb shoulder, cubed
  • 1 can chickpeas, rinsed
  • 1/2 head savoy cabbage, chopped
  • 1 preserved lemon, rind minced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cups chicken broth
  • 2 tsp ras el hanout
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 cup cilantro, chopped
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Brown the lamb in olive oil in a heavy pot, working in batches so it sears rather than steams.
  • Add the onion and cook 5 minutes, then stir in the ras el hanout.
  • Add the broth and chickpeas and simmer covered for 70 minutes.
  • Fold in the cabbage and preserved lemon and cook 12 minutes more.
  • Finish with a generous handful of cilantro.

Ingredients
  • 4 ahi tuna steaks (6 oz each)
  • 1/2 cup mixed sesame seeds
  • 1/2 oz dried wakame, rehydrated
  • 1 daikon radish, julienned
  • 1 cucumber, julienned
  • 3 Tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 Tbsp tamari
  • 1 Tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Soak the wakame in warm water 8 minutes, then drain and squeeze dry.
  • Whisk the rice vinegar, tamari, sesame oil, and ginger and toss with the wakame, daikon, and cucumber.
  • Press the sesame seeds firmly onto all sides of the tuna.
  • Sear in a very hot oiled pan for 75 seconds per side.
  • Rest 3 minutes, slice thin, and fan over the salad.

Ingredients
  • 3 acorn squash, halved and seeded
  • 1 lb ground turkey
  • 1 1/2 cups cooked wild rice
  • 1 cup chopped kale
  • 1/2 cup dried cranberries
  • 1/3 cup pecans, chopped
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp fresh sage, minced
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Rub the squash halves with olive oil and roast cut-side down at 400F for 35 minutes.
  • Brown the turkey with sage and cinnamon, about 7 minutes.
  • Stir in the wild rice, kale, and cranberries and cook 3 minutes.
  • Flip the squash over and fill each half with the mixture.
  • Top with pecans and bake 15 minutes more.

Ingredients
  • 1/2 cup sesame seeds, toasted
  • 1/2 cup sunflower seeds
  • 1 cup pitted dates
  • 2 Tbsp tahini
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt
  • 2 Tbsp unsweetened shredded coconut
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Pulse the dates in a food processor until they form a sticky paste.
  • Add the sesame seeds, sunflower seeds, tahini, vanilla, and salt and pulse to combine.
  • Roll tablespoon portions into balls.
  • Roll each one in shredded coconut.
  • Chill 30 minutes and store in the fridge for up to two weeks.

Ingredients
  • 1/2 cup Brazil nuts
  • 1/2 cup raw pumpkin seeds
  • 1 Tbsp coconut oil
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 2 apples, sliced thick
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toast the Brazil nuts and pumpkin seeds at 325F for 10 minutes.
  • Blend while still warm with coconut oil, salt, and cinnamon.
  • Keep blending for 4 to 5 minutes, scraping down, until it turns smooth and glossy.
  • Spread thickly on apple slices.
  • Store the extra butter in a jar in the fridge.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups plain whole-milk kefir
  • 1 1/2 tsp grass-fed gelatin
  • 2 Tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup mixed berries
  • 2 Tbsp ground flaxseed
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Sprinkle the gelatin over 1/4 cup of the kefir and let it bloom 5 minutes.
  • Warm 1/2 cup kefir with the maple syrup just until steaming, then whisk in the bloomed gelatin until dissolved.
  • Stir into the remaining cold kefir with the vanilla.
  • Pour into six small cups and chill 4 hours until set.
  • Top with berries and flaxseed before serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb edamame in the pod
  • 2 Tbsp white miso paste
  • 1 Tbsp mirin
  • 1 Tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1 Tbsp black sesame seeds
  • 1/2 tsp chili flakes
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Boil the edamame in salted water for 4 minutes and drain well.
  • Whisk the miso, mirin, sesame oil, and ginger into a glaze.
  • Toss the hot pods with the glaze so it clings.
  • Finish with black sesame seeds and chili flakes and serve warm.

Ingredients
  • 2 ripe avocados
  • 1/3 cup raw cacao powder
  • 1/3 cup maple syrup
  • 1/4 cup coconut milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt
  • 2 Tbsp cacao nibs
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Blend the avocados, cacao, maple syrup, coconut milk, vanilla, and salt until completely silky.
  • Scrape the sides and blend again so there are no green streaks.
  • Spoon into four small jars.
  • Chill at least 1 hour.
  • Top with cacao nibs just before eating.
