Focus: Mineral Replenish

This week's meals are built around Mineral Replenish. Each plate is designed to be whole-food, home-cook-friendly, and easy to prepare in 40 minutes or less.

Ingredients
  • 3 cups baby spinach
  • 4 eggs
  • 0.5 cup crumbled feta
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 0.25 tsp sea salt
  • 0.25 tsp black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat olive oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium heat.
  • Wilt spinach for 2 minutes, then season with sea salt and pepper.
  • Crack eggs over the spinach and scatter feta on top.
  • Bake at 375 F for 10 to 12 minutes until whites set.
  • Serve warm straight from the skillet.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup plain whole-milk yogurt
  • 2 Tbsp pumpkin seeds
  • 2 Tbsp sliced almonds
  • 0.5 cup blueberries
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 0.25 tsp cinnamon
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Spoon yogurt into a bowl.
  • Top with pumpkin seeds, almonds, and blueberries.
  • Drizzle with honey and dust with cinnamon.
  • Stir gently and serve chilled.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups diced sweet potato
  • 2 cups chopped kale
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp sea salt
  • 0.25 tsp smoked paprika
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
  • Cook sweet potato with sea salt for 12 minutes until tender.
  • Add kale and paprika, cook 3 more minutes until wilted.
  • Make two wells and crack in the eggs.
  • Cover and cook 4 minutes until eggs set, then serve.

Ingredients
  • 0.75 cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 1 sliced banana
  • 2 Tbsp chopped dark chocolate
  • 1 Tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 Tbsp sunflower seeds
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Combine oats, milk, and chia seeds in a jar.
  • Fold in banana and half the dark chocolate.
  • Cover and refrigerate overnight.
  • Top with remaining chocolate and sunflower seeds before serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 slices whole-grain bread
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 0.5 cup cooked white beans
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 0.25 tsp sea salt
  • 1 tsp pumpkin seeds
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toast the bread until golden.
  • Mash avocado and white beans with lemon juice and sea salt.
  • Spread the mixture over the toast.
  • Sprinkle pumpkin seeds on top and serve.

Ingredients
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 1 cup chopped Swiss chard
  • 1 Tbsp butter
  • 2 Tbsp grated Gruyere
  • 0.25 tsp sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Melt butter in a nonstick pan over medium heat.
  • Saute mushrooms and chard for 4 minutes until soft.
  • Whisk eggs with sea salt and pour into the pan.
  • Sprinkle Gruyere over one half as the eggs set.
  • Fold and slide onto a plate to serve.

Ingredients

Ingredients
  • 1.5 cups cooked chickpeas
  • 1 cup diced cucumber
  • 0.5 cup chopped parsley
  • 2 Tbsp tahini
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 Tbsp sunflower seeds
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk tahini with lemon juice and 2 Tbsp water until smooth.
  • Combine chickpeas, cucumber, and parsley in a bowl.
  • Pour the dressing over and toss.
  • Top with sunflower seeds and serve.

Ingredients
  • 4 cups bone broth
  • 2 Tbsp miso paste
  • 2 Tbsp dried wakame
  • 1 cup cubed tofu
  • 2 chopped green onions
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Warm the bone broth in a pot over medium heat.
  • Add wakame and tofu, simmer for 5 minutes.
  • Whisk miso paste with a ladle of broth, then stir it back in.
  • Remove from heat and add green onions and sesame oil.
  • Ladle into bowls and serve.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup cooked brown rice
  • 8 oz shrimp, peeled
  • 3 cups chopped Swiss chard
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 0.25 tsp sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
  • Saute garlic for 30 seconds, then add shrimp.
  • Cook shrimp 3 minutes until pink, then remove.
  • Wilt Swiss chard in the same pan for 3 minutes.
  • Serve shrimp and chard over brown rice with sea salt.

Ingredients
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 1.5 cups cooked white beans
  • 3 cups chopped kale
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1 diced carrot
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat olive oil in a pot and soften the carrot for 4 minutes.
  • Add broth, tomatoes, and white beans.
  • Simmer for 15 minutes.
  • Stir in kale and cook 5 more minutes.
  • Season and ladle into bowls.

Ingredients
  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 2 large sweet potatoes, cubed
  • 3 cups broccoli florets
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp sea salt
  • 1 tsp dried dill
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 425 F.
  • Toss sweet potato and broccoli with 1 Tbsp olive oil and sea salt on a sheet pan.
  • Roast for 15 minutes.
  • Add salmon, brush with remaining oil, and sprinkle with dill.
  • Roast 12 more minutes until salmon flakes, then serve.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb grass-fed beef strips
  • 2 cups sliced mushrooms
  • 3 cups baby spinach
  • 2 Tbsp tamari
  • 1 Tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 Tbsp sesame oil
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat sesame oil in a wok over high heat.
  • Sear beef strips for 3 minutes, then remove.
  • Saute mushrooms and ginger for 4 minutes.
  • Return beef, add tamari and spinach, toss 2 minutes.
  • Serve hot.

Ingredients
  • 2 lb mussels, cleaned
  • 2 cups diced tomatoes
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup vegetable broth
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 0.25 cup chopped parsley
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat olive oil in a large pot and saute garlic for 1 minute.
  • Add tomatoes and broth, simmer 5 minutes.
  • Add mussels, cover, and steam 6 minutes until they open.
  • Discard any unopened mussels.
  • Sprinkle with parsley and serve with broth.

Ingredients
  • 1.5 cups dried red lentils
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 4 cups chopped spinach
  • 2 Tbsp curry powder
  • 1 diced onion
  • 1 Tbsp coconut oil
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Melt coconut oil in a pot and soften onion for 4 minutes.
  • Stir in curry powder and cook 1 minute.
  • Add lentils, coconut milk, and 2 cups water.
  • Simmer 18 minutes until lentils are tender.
  • Fold in spinach and cook 3 minutes, then serve.

Ingredients
  • 6 bone-in chicken thighs
  • 1.5 cups cooked cannellini beans
  • 4 cups chopped Swiss chard
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp sea salt
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 425 F.
  • Rub chicken thighs with 1 Tbsp olive oil, sea salt, and paprika.
  • Roast on a sheet pan for 25 minutes.
  • Saute chard and beans in remaining oil for 5 minutes.
  • Serve chicken over the chard and beans.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb sea scallops
  • 3 cups cubed butternut squash
  • 2 Tbsp butter
  • 2 cups arugula
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Boil butternut squash for 15 minutes until soft, then drain.
  • Mash squash with butter and half the sea salt.
  • Pat scallops dry and season with remaining salt.
  • Sear scallops in olive oil for 2 minutes per side.
  • Plate puree, top with scallops and arugula, then serve.
