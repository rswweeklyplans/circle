Focus: Simplify & Prep

This week's meals are built around Simplify & Prep. Each plate is designed to be whole-food, home-cook-friendly, and easy to prepare in 40 minutes or less.

Ingredients
  • 12 pasture-raised eggs
  • 1/2 cup whole milk
  • 3 cups small broccoli florets, steamed 3 minutes
  • 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar
  • 1/2 small onion, finely diced
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 Tbsp butter for the dish
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the oven to 375F and butter a 9 by 13 baking dish.
  • Whisk the eggs with the milk, Dijon, salt, and pepper.
  • Scatter the broccoli, onion, and most of the cheese in the dish and pour the eggs over.
  • Top with the remaining cheese and bake 28 to 32 minutes until set in the center.
  • Cool, cut into 8 squares, and refrigerate up to 5 days. Reheat 60 seconds in the microwave.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • 2 cups unsweetened almond milk
  • 1 cup canned pumpkin puree
  • 1 cup whole-milk Greek yogurt
  • 2 Tbsp maple syrup
  • 2 Tbsp chia seeds
  • 2 tsp pumpkin pie spice
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup chopped pecans
  • Pinch of sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk the almond milk, pumpkin, yogurt, maple syrup, spice, vanilla, and salt in a large bowl.
  • Stir in the oats and chia seeds.
  • Divide between 4 jars and refrigerate overnight.
  • Top with pecans before eating. Keeps 4 days.

Ingredients
  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 2 pasture-raised eggs
  • 2 1/2 cups rolled oats
  • 1/4 cup natural peanut butter
  • 1/4 cup maple syrup
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt
  • 1/3 cup dark chocolate chips or blueberries
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the oven to 350F and line a 12-cup muffin tin.
  • Blend everything except the chocolate chips until smooth.
  • Stir in the chocolate chips and divide the batter between the cups.
  • Bake 18 to 20 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean.
  • Cool and store in the fridge 5 days or freeze for a month.

Ingredients
  • 6 pasture-raised eggs
  • 2 Tbsp whole milk
  • 6 slices turkey bacon
  • 6 whole-grain English muffins, split
  • 6 slices sharp cheddar
  • 1 cup baby spinach
  • Cooking spray or butter
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the oven to 350F and grease a 9 by 13 baking dish.
  • Whisk the eggs with milk, salt, and pepper, pour into the dish, scatter the spinach on top, and bake 15 minutes until set.
  • Meanwhile, cook the turkey bacon in a skillet until crisp.
  • Cut the eggs into 6 squares and build each sandwich with egg, bacon, and cheese.
  • Wrap individually in parchment and foil and freeze. Reheat unwrapped 90 seconds in the microwave.

Ingredients
  • 3 cups frozen mixed berries
  • 3 cups baby spinach
  • 3 bananas, sliced
  • 6 Tbsp hemp hearts
  • 6 scoops vanilla protein powder
  • Unsweetened almond milk for blending
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Divide the berries, spinach, banana, and hemp hearts between 6 freezer bags.
  • Add 1 scoop of protein to each bag, press out the air, and freeze flat.
  • To serve, dump one bag into a blender with 1 cup almond milk.
  • Blend until smooth. Packs keep 2 months.

Ingredients
  • 1/2 cup chia seeds
  • 2 cups unsweetened almond milk
  • 1/2 cup whole-milk Greek yogurt
  • 2 Tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 apples, diced
  • 1 Tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup chopped walnuts
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk the almond milk, yogurt, maple syrup, 1 tsp cinnamon, and vanilla, then stir in the chia seeds.
  • Let sit 10 minutes, whisk again, and divide between 4 jars.
  • Cook the apples in butter with the remaining cinnamon 5 to 6 minutes until soft.
  • Spoon the apples over the pudding and top with walnuts.
  • Refrigerate overnight. Keeps 4 days.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 lb boneless chicken thighs
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups cooked quinoa or brown rice
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup kalamata olives
  • 1/2 cup feta, crumbled
  • 1 cup whole-milk Greek yogurt mixed with 1 clove garlic and 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toss the chicken with 2 Tbsp olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, garlic, salt, and pepper.
  • Heat a skillet over medium-high and cook the chicken 6 to 7 minutes per side, then rest and slice.
  • Toss the cucumber, tomatoes, onion, and olives with the remaining olive oil and a pinch of salt.
  • Divide the quinoa between 4 containers and top with chicken, salad, and feta.
  • Pack the garlic yogurt separately. Keeps 4 days.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup corn kernels

  • 1 red bell pepper, diced

Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk the olive oil, lime juice, cumin, honey, salt, and pepper and divide between 4 wide-mouth jars.
  • Layer in the black beans, corn, tomatoes, and bell pepper.
  • Add the quinoa and cilantro, then the cheese.
  • Pack the romaine on top and seal.

Ingredients
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 6 cups low-sodium vegetable or chicken broth
  • 1 can (15 oz) cannellini beans, drained
  • 1 cup ditalini or small whole-grain pasta
  • 1 zucchini, diced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 3 cups chopped kale
  • Grated Parmesan for serving
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the olive oil in a large pot and cook the onion, carrots, and celery 6 minutes.
  • Add the garlic, oregano, and basil and cook 1 minute.
  • Add the tomatoes, broth, and beans and bring to a boil.
  • Stir in the pasta and zucchini and simmer 10 minutes.
  • Add the kale and cook 3 minutes, then season and serve with Parmesan. Freezes well.

Ingredients
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) chickpeas, drained and patted dry
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 cup whole-milk Greek yogurt
  • 1 clove garlic, grated
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 4 whole-grain wraps
  • 2 cups shredded romaine
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the oven to 425F and toss the chickpeas with olive oil, cumin, paprika, coriander, turmeric, cinnamon, salt, and pepper.
  • Roast 20 to 25 minutes until crisp at the edges.
  • Stir the yogurt with garlic, lemon juice, and salt.
  • Warm the wraps and fill with romaine, cucumber, tomato, onion, and chickpeas.
  • Drizzle with garlic yogurt and roll. Store components separately for the week.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/4 lb flank steak, thinly sliced against the grain
  • 1/4 cup coconut aminos
  • 1 Tbsp honey
  • 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 Tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbsp arrowroot or cornstarch
  • 2 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 5 cups broccoli florets
  • 3 cups cooked brown rice
  • 1 Tbsp sesame seeds
  • 2 scallions, sliced
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk the coconut aminos, honey, rice vinegar, ginger, garlic, and arrowroot.
  • Heat 1 Tbsp avocado oil in a large skillet over high heat and sear the beef 2 to 3 minutes, then remove.
  • Add the remaining oil and the broccoli with 2 Tbsp water, cover, and steam 4 minutes.
  • Return the beef, pour in the sauce, and toss 2 minutes until glossy.
  • Divide the rice between 4 containers, top with beef and broccoli, sesame seeds, and scallions. Keeps 4 days.

Ingredients
  • 4 medium sweet potatoes
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, rinsed
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp chili powder
  • 1 cup salsa
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 1/2 cup whole-milk Greek yogurt
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheddar
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • Salt to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Pierce the sweet potatoes and microwave 8 to 10 minutes until tender, or bake at 425F for 40 minutes on prep day.
  • Warm the black beans with cumin, chili powder, and a pinch of salt.
  • Split the potatoes and fluff the insides.
  • Top with beans, salsa, cheddar, avocado, yogurt, and scallions.
  • Finish with lime juice. Bake potatoes ahead and reheat for fast lunches.

Ingredients
  • 8 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 3 Tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 Tbsp raw honey
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 1/2 lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 1 lb carrots, cut into sticks
  • Fresh parsley for serving
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the oven to 425F.
  • Toss the potatoes and carrots with 2 Tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper on a sheet pan and roast 10 minutes.
  • Whisk the Dijon, honey, remaining olive oil, garlic, thyme, salt, and pepper and coat the chicken.
  • Nestle the chicken among the vegetables and roast 22 to 25 minutes until 165F.
  • Top with parsley. Leftovers reheat well for lunch.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 lb boneless chicken thighs, cut into chunks
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 1/2 cups long-grain white or jasmine rice, rinsed
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • Zest and juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 1/2 cups frozen peas
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley or dill, chopped
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the olive oil in a large pot and brown the chicken with salt and pepper 5 minutes, then remove.
  • Cook the onion 4 minutes, then add the garlic and oregano for 1 minute.
  • Stir in the rice, broth, lemon zest, and chicken, bring to a boil, cover, and simmer on low 15 minutes.
  • Scatter the peas on top, cover, and rest off the heat 5 minutes.
  • Fluff with lemon juice and herbs.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb shelf-stable potato gnocchi
  • 12 oz precooked chicken sausage, sliced
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes
  • 2 zucchini, cut into half moons
  • 1 red onion, cut into wedges
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1/3 cup grated Parmesan
  • Fresh basil for serving
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the oven to 450F.
  • Toss the gnocchi, sausage, tomatoes, zucchini, and onion with olive oil, Italian seasoning, pepper flakes, salt, and pepper on a large sheet pan.
  • Roast 20 to 22 minutes, stirring once, until the gnocchi are crisp and the tomatoes burst.
  • Top with Parmesan and basil and serve.

Ingredients
  • 4 wild salmon fillets (6 oz each)
  • 2 Tbsp harissa paste
  • 1 Tbsp raw honey
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 1/2 lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 1 lb green beans, trimmed
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
  • 1/2 cup whole-milk Greek yogurt
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the oven to 425F and roast the potatoes with 2 Tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper 15 minutes.
  • Mix the harissa, honey, and remaining olive oil and brush over the salmon.
  • Push the potatoes aside, add the green beans and salmon to the pan, and roast 12 minutes until the fish flakes.
  • Serve with lemon wedges and a spoon of yogurt to cool the heat.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb frozen wild shrimp, thawed and patted dry
  • 3 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 3 pasture-raised eggs, beaten
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 3 cups frozen peas and carrots
  • 4 cups cold cooked rice, ideally day-old
  • 3 Tbsp coconut aminos or tamari
  • 1 Tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 3 scallions, sliced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat 1 Tbsp avocado oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat and cook the shrimp 2 minutes per side, then remove.
  • Add 1 Tbsp oil and scramble the eggs, then push to the side.
  • Add the remaining oil, garlic, ginger, and frozen vegetables and stir-fry 3 minutes.
  • Add the rice and coconut aminos and toss 4 to 5 minutes until heated and lightly crisp.
  • Return the shrimp, finish with sesame oil and scallions, and season.
