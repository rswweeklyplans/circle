Focus: Metabolic Flexibility

This week's meals are built around Metabolic Flexibility. Each plate is designed to be whole-food, home-cook-friendly, and easy to prepare in 40 minutes or less.

Ingredients
  • 4 pasture-raised eggs
  • 3 oz chicken sausage, sliced
  • 1 cup chopped kale
  • 1/4 cup diced onion
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp grated parmesan
  • Sea salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 375F.
  • Brown sausage and onion in an oven-safe skillet.
  • Add kale and wilt, then pour in whisked eggs.
  • Top with parmesan and bake 12-14 minutes.
