# Build & Restore: program

Approved 2026-10-04. The 7 week files (`build_restore_week1.html` to `build_restore_week7.html`) were generated from this version.

**How to review:** change anything directly in this file, or tell Jeff the changes. Exercise names, home swaps, sets, reps, cues, the body-care routines and all client-facing copy are yours to edit. Section 8 lists the four decisions that most need your call.

## 1. The block at a glance

- **Goal:** build muscle (hypertrophy) with 3 lifting days, and care for the body on the other 4.
- **Length:** 6 weeks, then a week 7 deload.
- **Rep ranges:** big lifts 8-12, accessories 10-12, isolation 12-15.
- **Sets:** straight sets, no supersets.
- **Rest:** about 90 seconds on the big lifts, 60 seconds on everything else.
- **Access:** no access code. Each week opens directly from its link.
- **Mini session:** removed for this block. The four guided body-care days replace it.

### Weekly layout

| Day | Type | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Lift | Lower Body |
| Day 2 | Body care | Hips and Low Back Mobility |
| Day 3 | Lift | Upper Body |
| Day 4 | Body care | Shoulders and Upper Back Opening |
| Day 5 | Lift | Full Body |
| Day 6 | Body care | Long Walk and Full-Body Stretch |
| Day 7 | Body care | Nervous System Downshift |

Each body-care day follows the lifting day it helps recover from. Day 7 is the quietest day of the week.

### Progression across the block

| Weeks | Focus | Effort on working sets |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Find working weights. Form over load. | Leave 2-3 reps in reserve |
| 3-4 | Add load or reps compared with the previous weeks | Leave 1-2 reps in reserve |
| 5-6 | Push intensity. The last set should be a grind. | Last set 0-1 reps in reserve |
| 7 | Deload: 2 sets of everything, 10-20% lighter | Leave 4 or more reps in reserve |

## 4. Body-care days

## 5. Weekly copy

### Goal box (one per week)

| Week | Title | Text |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Goal of Week 1: Find Your Weights | New block, new rep ranges. Pick weights you can control for every rep and leave 2-3 reps in reserve. Focus on form over load. |
| 2 | Goal of Week 2: Lock It In | Repeat the same lifts and confirm your working weights. Still 2-3 reps in reserve. Do all four body-care days this week. |
| 3 | Goal of Week 3: Start Adding | Add load or reps compared with weeks 1 and 2. When every set reaches the top of the rep range with good form, add 2.5-10 lb. |
| 4 | Goal of Week 4: Keep Building | Keep the progress going with small jumps in load or one more rep per set. Leave 1-2 reps in reserve. |
| 5 | Goal of Week 5: Push the Intensity | The last set of each exercise should be a grind. Push close to failure while keeping every rep clean. |
| 6 | Goal of Week 6: Finish Strong | Final building week. Take your last sets close to failure, log your numbers, and aim for your best weights of the block. |
| 7 | Goal of Deload Week: Recover and Absorb | Same exercises, less work. Do 2 sets of everything at about 10-20% less than your Week 6 weights, and finish every set with 4 or more reps in reserve. |

### Standing notes

- **Warm-up note (lifting days):** Warm up for 5-7 minutes first: easy walk or bike, then one light set of your first two exercises. Rest about 90 seconds on the big lifts and 60 seconds on everything else.
- **Phase note:** This phase: big lifts 8-12 reps, accessories 10-12, isolation 12-15. When all sets reach the top of the rep range with good form, add 2.5-10 lb. **Training at home?** Lower every rep for 3 slow seconds and add reps (up to 15-20) so lighter dumbbells still take you close to your limit.
- **Deload phase note:** Deload rules: same exercises and rep ranges, 2 sets each, about 10-20% lighter than your Week 6 weights, 4 or more reps in reserve on every set (RPE 5-6), no challenge sets. **Training at home?** Keep your usual dumbbells and stop each set 4-5 reps earlier than you normally would.
- **Weekly walking note:** Walk every day in this block. On lifting days, 10-20 easy minutes is enough. On body-care days, follow that day's walk target. Aim for 7,000-10,000 steps a day. Walking supports recovery, steadies your mood and helps you sleep.
- **Deload walking note:** Keep your daily walks this week and keep them easy. Easy walking speeds up recovery without adding fatigue.

## 6. Deload week (week 7)

- Same exercises and rep ranges, 2 sets each. That makes 14 sets on Day 1, 16 on Day 3 and 14 on Day 5.
- About 10-20% lighter than Week 6. Every set ends with 4 or more reps in reserve.
- No Rachel Challenge Sets.
- Push-ups: about half the reps you hit in Week 6.
- Body-care days are unchanged.

## 8. Decisions for Rachel

1. **Exercise selection.** Are these the right lifts for your clients, and are the home swaps realistic with dumbbells, a band and a bench or couch?
2. **Volume.** Day 3 is the longest session at 8 exercises and 25 sets. Cutting the biceps and triceps work brings it to 6 exercises and 21 sets, with no effect on the priority muscle groups.
3. **Body-care routines.** Are these the moves, holds and breathing patterns you would teach? Day 7 in particular should sound like you.
4. **Challenge sets.** Hip thrust on Day 1, lateral raise on Day 3, single-leg hip thrust on Day 5. Keep, change or drop any of them.
