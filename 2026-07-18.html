Focus: Hormone Harmony

This week's meals are built around Hormone Harmony. Each plate is designed to be whole-food, home-cook-friendly, and easy to prepare in 40 minutes or less.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup chickpea flour
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 cups chopped kale
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 0.25 tsp turmeric
  • 0.25 tsp black pepper
  • 0.5 tsp sea salt
  • 2 Tbsp nutritional yeast
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk chickpea flour, water, turmeric, pepper, salt, and yeast into a smooth batter.
  • Heat olive oil in a skillet and pour in the batter.
  • Add kale and stir constantly until the batter sets into soft curds, about 6 minutes.
  • Serve warm topped with extra cracked pepper.

Ingredients
  • 0.5 cup buckwheat groats
  • 1.5 cups water
  • 0.75 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 2 Tbsp pumpkin seeds
  • 1 Tbsp ground flax
  • 0.5 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp raw honey
  • 0.5 cup blueberries
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Simmer buckwheat groats in water for about 15 minutes until tender.
  • Stir in almond milk, flax, and cinnamon and cook 3 more minutes.
  • Spoon into a bowl and top with blueberries and pumpkin seeds.
  • Drizzle with honey and serve warm.

Ingredients
  • 6 pasture eggs
  • 3 oz smoked wild salmon, chopped
  • 1 cup chopped spinach
  • 2 Tbsp chopped fresh dill
  • 0.25 tsp sea salt
  • 0.25 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp olive oil
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 375 F and oil a 6-cup muffin tin.
  • Whisk eggs with salt, pepper, and dill in a bowl.
  • Fold in spinach and salmon, then divide among the muffin cups.
  • Bake 18 minutes until set and serve warm.

Ingredients
  • 1 medium sweet potato, diced
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 2 pasture eggs
  • 1 cup arugula
  • 1 Tbsp sunflower seeds
  • 0.25 tsp smoked paprika
  • 0.25 tsp sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toss sweet potato with olive oil, paprika, and salt and roast at 425 F for 20 minutes.
  • Fry or soft-boil the eggs while the potato roasts.
  • Build a bowl with arugula, roasted sweet potato, and avocado.
  • Top with eggs and sunflower seeds and serve.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups riced cauliflower
  • 1 cup full-fat coconut milk
  • 1 Tbsp chia seeds
  • 0.5 tsp vanilla extract
  • 0.5 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 Tbsp maple syrup
  • 2 Tbsp chopped walnuts
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Simmer riced cauliflower with coconut milk for 8 minutes until soft.
  • Stir in chia, vanilla, cinnamon, and maple syrup.
  • Cook 3 more minutes until it thickens.
  • Top with walnuts and serve warm or chilled.

Ingredients

  • 0.33 cup crumbled feta
  • 2 Tbsp chopped basil

  • 0.25 tsp sea salt
  • 0.25 tsp black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat olive oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium heat.

  • 2 Tbsp chopped parsley

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp grated ginger

  • 2 Tbsp sesame seeds
  • 0.5 tsp sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe

Ingredients
  • 12 oz wild cod fillet
  • 1 head cauliflower, chopped
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp ghee
  • 1 Tbsp capers
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 Tbsp chopped chives
  • 0.5 tsp sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Steam cauliflower until very tender, then blend with ghee and half the salt into a puree.
  • Rub cod with olive oil, lemon juice, and remaining salt.
  • Bake cod at 400 F for 14 minutes until it flakes.
  • Spoon puree onto plates, top with cod, capers, and chives.
