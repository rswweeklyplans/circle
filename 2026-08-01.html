Focus: Micronutrient Density

This week's meals are built around Micronutrient Density. Each plate is designed to be whole-food, home-cook-friendly, and easy to prepare in 40 minutes or less.

Ingredients
  • 4 pasture eggs
  • 1 cup chopped red bell pepper
  • 1 cup baby spinach
  • 0.5 cup diced red onion
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 0.25 tsp sea salt
  • 0.25 cup crumbled feta
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
  • Saute onion and bell pepper for 4 minutes until soft.
  • Add spinach and cook until wilted, about 1 minute.
  • Whisk eggs with salt, pour in, and scramble 2 to 3 minutes.
  • Top with feta and serve warm.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup frozen wild blueberries
  • 0.5 cup cooked diced beet
  • 0.5 cup plain whole milk yogurt
  • 0.5 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 1 Tbsp chia seeds
  • 2 Tbsp sliced almonds
  • 1 tsp raw honey
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Blend blueberries, beet, yogurt, almond milk, and honey until smooth.
  • Pour into a bowl.
  • Sprinkle chia seeds and sliced almonds over top.
  • Serve immediately.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups diced sweet potato
  • 2 cups chopped kale
  • 2 pasture eggs
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp smoked paprika
  • 0.25 tsp sea salt
  • 0.25 cup diced red onion
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat 1 Tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
  • Cook sweet potato with paprika and salt for 12 minutes, stirring.
  • Add onion and kale and cook 4 more minutes until tender.
  • In a second pan, fry eggs in remaining oil to desired doneness.
  • Plate hash and top with fried eggs.

Ingredients
  • 3 Tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 0.5 tsp vanilla extract
  • 0.5 cup pomegranate seeds
  • 1 diced kiwi
  • 1 tsp maple syrup
  • 2 Tbsp chopped walnuts
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Stir chia seeds, coconut milk, vanilla, and maple syrup in a jar.
  • Refrigerate at least 20 minutes until thickened.
  • Top with pomegranate seeds, kiwi, and walnuts.
  • Serve chilled.

Ingredients
  • 6 pasture eggs

  • 1 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 0.25 cup grated parmesan
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 0.25 tsp sea salt
  • 0.25 cup diced tomato
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Preheat oven to 375 F and oil a muffin tin.

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup mixed berries

  • 1 Tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 0.5 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp raw honey

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup cherry tomatoes

Ingredients
  • 6 oz chicken breast
  • 2 cups mixed greens
  • 0.5 cup diced roasted beet
  • 0.25 cup crumbled goat cheese
  • 2 Tbsp chopped walnuts
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp balsamic vinegar
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Season chicken and grill 6 minutes per side until cooked through.
  • Slice chicken and let it rest.
  • Arrange greens, beet, goat cheese, and walnuts on a plate.
  • Whisk olive oil and balsamic and drizzle over the salad.
  • Top with sliced chicken.

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbsp olive oil

Prep Notes / Recipe

Ingredients
  • 5 oz wild cod fillet
  • 2 cups broccolini
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves minced garlic
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • 0.25 tsp sea salt
  • 0.25 tsp red pepper flakes
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Pat cod dry and season with salt.
  • Sear cod in 1 Tbsp olive oil for 4 minutes per side.

  • Plate cod over broccolini and finish with lemon zest.
