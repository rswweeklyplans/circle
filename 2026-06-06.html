Focus: Hormone Harmony

This week's meals are built around Hormone Harmony. Each plate is designed to be whole-food, home-cook-friendly, and easy to prepare in 40 minutes or less.

Ingredients
  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 2 Tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 3/4 cup almond milk
  • 1/2 cup mixed berries
  • 1 Tbsp almond butter
  • 1 tsp raw honey
  • 1/4 tsp cinnamon
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Stir oats, flax, almond milk, cinnamon, and honey in a jar.
  • Top with berries and a swirl of almond butter.
  • Cover and refrigerate overnight.
  • Stir and enjoy cold or warmed.

Ingredients
  • 2 pasture-raised eggs
  • 1/2 avocado, sliced
  • 1 cup baby spinach
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp pumpkin seeds
  • Sea salt and pepper to taste
  • Pinch red pepper flakes
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Soft-boil eggs for 6-7 minutes, then peel and halve.
  • Saute spinach in olive oil until just wilted.
  • Plate spinach, avocado, and eggs.
  • Top with pumpkin seeds, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes.

Ingredients
  • 3 pasture-raised eggs
  • 2 oz smoked wild salmon
  • 1 Tbsp chopped chives
  • 1 tsp grass-fed butter
  • 1 Tbsp goat cheese
  • Black pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk eggs with a pinch of pepper.
  • Melt butter in a nonstick pan over low heat.
  • Add eggs and gently fold until soft-set.
  • Fold in flaked salmon and goat cheese off heat.
  • Garnish with chives.

Ingredients
  • 3 Tbsp chia seeds
  • 3/4 cup coconut milk
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp maple syrup
  • 2 Tbsp pumpkin seeds
  • 1/4 cup raspberries
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk chia, coconut milk, vanilla, and maple syrup.
  • Let sit 5 minutes, then whisk again to prevent clumps.
  • Refrigerate at least 2 hours or overnight.
  • Top with pumpkin seeds and raspberries before serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup diced sweet potato
  • 4 oz ground turkey
  • 1/2 cup diced bell pepper
  • 1/4 cup diced onion
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • Sea salt to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Saute sweet potato in olive oil over medium heat 8 minutes.
  • Add onion and bell pepper; cook 4 minutes.
  • Push veg aside and brown the turkey with paprika and salt.
  • Combine everything and cook until potato is tender.

Ingredients
  • 4 pasture-raised eggs
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 2 Tbsp crumbled goat cheese
  • 1/4 cup diced tomato
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • Sea salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 375F.
  • Saute spinach and tomato in an oven-safe skillet with olive oil.
  • Whisk eggs with salt and pepper and pour over veg.
  • Dot with goat cheese and cook 2 minutes on stovetop.
  • Transfer to oven and bake 10-12 minutes until set.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup canned chickpeas, rinsed
  • 1 cup chopped broccoli
  • 1/2 cup chopped red onion
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cups mixed greens
  • 1 Tbsp tahini
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 425F.
  • Toss broccoli, onion, and chickpeas with 1 Tbsp olive oil; roast 20 minutes.
  • Whisk tahini, lemon juice, and remaining oil with a splash of water.
  • Serve roasted veg over greens with dressing.

Ingredients
  • 3/4 cup dried green lentils
  • 3 cups vegetable broth
  • 2 cups chopped kale
  • 1/2 cup diced carrot
  • 1/2 cup diced onion
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Saute onion, carrot, and garlic in olive oil 5 minutes.
  • Add lentils and broth; bring to a boil.
  • Simmer 25 minutes until lentils are tender.
  • Stir in kale and cook 3 more minutes.
  • Season with salt and pepper.

Ingredients
  • 5 oz cod fillet
  • 2 Tbsp chopped olives
  • 1 Tbsp capers
  • 2 cups sliced zucchini
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 400F.
  • Mix olives, capers, lemon zest, and 1 Tbsp oil for tapenade.
  • Place cod on a lined sheet and top with tapenade.
  • Toss zucchini with remaining oil and garlic; add to pan.
  • Roast 15 minutes until fish flakes easily.

Ingredients
  • 6 oz ground turkey
  • 1 small spaghetti squash
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup almond flour
  • 1 cup marinara sauce
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 400F. Halve squash, brush with 1 Tbsp oil, roast cut-side down 35 minutes.
  • Mix turkey, egg, almond flour, and seasoning; form meatballs.
  • Brown meatballs in remaining oil, then simmer in marinara 10 minutes.
  • Scrape squash into strands and top with meatballs and sauce.
