Focus: Simplify & Prep

This week's meals are built around Simplify & Prep. Each plate is designed to be whole-food, home-cook-friendly, and easy to prepare in 40 minutes or less.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 Tbsp chia seeds
  • 2 Tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup blueberries
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Stir oats, milk, yogurt, chia, maple, and vanilla in a large bowl.
  • Divide into 4 mason jars.
  • Top each jar with blueberries.
  • Seal and chill overnight, up to 4 days.

Ingredients
  • 10 eggs
  • 1 cup chopped spinach
  • 1 cup diced bell pepper
  • 0.5 cup shredded cheddar
  • 0.5 tsp salt
  • 0.25 tsp black pepper
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 375 F and oil a 12-cup muffin tin.
  • Whisk eggs with salt and pepper.
  • Stir in spinach, bell pepper, and cheddar.
  • Pour into muffin cups.
  • Bake 20 minutes until set, then cool and refrigerate.

Ingredients
  • 1.5 lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 1 lb chicken sausage, sliced
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 0.5 tsp garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp salt
  • 1 cup diced onion
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 425 F.
  • Toss potatoes, sausage, and onion with oil and spices on a sheet pan.
  • Spread in a single layer.
  • Roast 25 minutes, stirring once.
  • Portion into containers for the week.

Ingredients
  • 3 bananas, sliced
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 4 Tbsp peanut butter
  • 2 Tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 3 cups whole milk
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Divide bananas, oats, peanut butter, and flax into 3 freezer bags.
  • Freeze flat.
  • In the morning, empty one bag into a blender with 1 cup milk.
  • Blend until smooth and serve.

Ingredients
  • 6 eggs
  • 4 slices whole-grain bread
  • 1 avocado
  • 0.25 tsp salt
  • 0.25 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Boil eggs 10 minutes, then cool in ice water and peel.
  • Store peeled eggs in the fridge.
  • Toast bread and mash avocado with lemon and salt.
  • Spread avocado on toast and top with sliced eggs and pepper flakes.

Ingredients
  • 3 cups plain Greek yogurt
  • 1.5 cups granola
  • 1 cup sliced strawberries
  • 3 Tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Stir honey and cinnamon into the yogurt.
  • Layer yogurt, strawberries, and granola in 3 jars.
  • Add granola last to keep it crunchy.
  • Seal and refrigerate up to 3 days.

Ingredients
  • 2.5 cups rolled oats
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 apple, diced
  • 3 Tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp baking powder
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 375 F and oil an 8x8 baking dish.
  • Whisk milk, eggs, and maple syrup.
  • Stir in oats, apple, cinnamon, and baking powder.
  • Pour into the dish and bake 30 minutes.
  • Cool, cut into squares, and store for grab-and-go mornings.

Ingredients
  • 2 cans white beans, drained
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 3 cups chopped kale
  • 1 cup diced onion
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Warm olive oil in a large pot and cook onion and garlic 4 minutes.
  • Add beans, broth, and Italian seasoning.
  • Simmer 15 minutes.
  • Stir in kale and cook 5 more minutes.
  • Cool and portion into jars for grab-and-heat lunches.

  • 3 carrots, chopped

  • 1 cup diced onion
  • 2 Tbsp tomato paste

Prep Notes / Recipe
