Focus: Anti-Inflammatory Foods

This week's meals are built around foods that calm inflammation and support whole-body recovery. Plates feature omega-3 rich fatty fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel), extra-virgin olive oil, colorful berries and leafy greens, turmeric and ginger, and a handful of nuts and seeds at every meal. Each recipe is whole-food, home-cook-friendly, and ready in 40 minutes or less.

Ingredients
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 0.5 cup frozen mango
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 0.5 tsp fresh grated ginger
  • 1 Tbsp almond butter
  • 2 Tbsp hemp seeds
  • 0.25 cup blueberries
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Blend banana, mango, almond milk, turmeric, ginger, and almond butter until thick
  • Pour into a bowl
  • Top with hemp seeds and blueberries
  • Serve immediately

Ingredients

Prep Notes / Recipe

Ingredients
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 1 cup sliced cremini mushrooms
  • 0.25 cup diced shallot
  • 2 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 0.5 avocado sliced
  • salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Preheat broiler and heat olive oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium heat
  • Sauté shallot and mushrooms 5 minutes until golden
  • Add spinach and stir until wilted
  • Whisk eggs with salt and pepper, pour into pan and cook 3 minutes until edges set
  • Broil 2-3 minutes until puffed and golden; top with avocado

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 yellow onion diced
  • 3 cloves garlic minced
  • 1 Tbsp fresh grated ginger
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 2 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 0.5 cup full-fat coconut milk
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat olive oil in a pot and sauté onion 4 minutes until softened
