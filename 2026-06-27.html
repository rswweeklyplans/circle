Focus: Gut Health & Fiber

This week's meals are built around Gut Health & Fiber. Each plate is designed to be whole-food, home-cook-friendly, and easy to prepare in 40 minutes or less.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup plain kefir
  • 1/2 cup mixed berries
  • 1 Tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 1 Tbsp chia seeds
  • 1/2 banana
  • 1 cup spinach

Ingredients

Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Stir oats, kefir, chia, and cinnamon in a jar.

  • Top with kiwi and almonds before eating.

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbsp chopped walnuts
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp ground flaxseed
Prep Notes / Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp grated ginger

Prep Notes / Recipe

  • Sprinkle with sesame seeds.
