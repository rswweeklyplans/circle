Focus: Recovery & Strength

This week's meals are built around Recovery & Strength. Each plate is designed to be whole-food, home-cook-friendly, and easy to prepare in 40 minutes or less.

Ingredients
  • 6 oz grass-fed sirloin, diced
  • 1 medium sweet potato, diced
  • 2 pasture-raised eggs
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/4 tsp sea salt
  • 1 handful baby spinach
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
  • Add sweet potato and cook 8 minutes until tender.
  • Add steak and paprika, cook 4 minutes to sear.
  • Stir in spinach until wilted, then season with salt.
  • Fry eggs in the same pan and serve on top.

Ingredients
  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 3/4 cup whole milk
  • 2 Tbsp collagen peptides
  • 1/2 cup mixed berries
  • 1 Tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1/4 tsp cinnamon
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Stir oats, milk, collagen, and chia seeds in a jar.
  • Add cinnamon and honey and mix well.
  • Cover and refrigerate overnight.
  • Top with fresh berries before eating.

Ingredients
  • 1/2 cup cottage cheese
  • 2 pasture-raised eggs
  • 1/2 cup oat flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 Tbsp butter
  • 1/2 cup sliced banana
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Blend cottage cheese, eggs, oat flour, baking powder, and vanilla.
  • Melt butter in a skillet over medium heat.
  • Pour batter into small rounds and cook 2 minutes per side.
  • Serve topped with sliced banana.

Ingredients
  • 2 slices sprouted whole grain bread
  • 4 oz wild smoked salmon
  • 1/2 avocado, mashed
  • 2 tsp capers
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 Tbsp fresh dill
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toast the bread until golden.
  • Spread mashed avocado over each slice and add lemon juice.
  • Layer smoked salmon on top.
  • Scatter capers, dill, and black pepper to finish.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt

Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Spoon Greek yogurt into a bowl.

Ingredients
  • 6 pasture-raised eggs
  • 1/2 cup diced bell pepper
  • 1/2 cup chopped spinach
  • 1/4 cup crumbled feta
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1/4 tsp sea salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Preheat oven to 375 F and grease a muffin tin with olive oil.
  • Whisk eggs with salt and pepper.
  • Divide bell pepper, spinach, and feta among the cups.
  • Pour egg over the vegetables.
  • Bake 18 minutes until set.

Ingredients
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 2 Tbsp almond butter
  • 1 scoop whey protein
  • 1 Tbsp cacao powder
  • 1/2 cup ice
  • 1 pinch sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Add banana, milk, and almond butter to a blender.
  • Add whey protein, cacao powder, and salt.
  • Add ice and blend until smooth.
  • Pour into a glass and serve.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup chopped kale
  • 1/2 avocado, sliced

  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Massage kale with 1 Tbsp olive oil and lemon juice.

Ingredients
  • 5 oz wild salmon fillet

  • 1/2 cup green beans, blanched
  • 6 small boiled potatoes, halved

  • 2 Tbsp olive oil

Prep Notes / Recipe

Ingredients
  • 6 oz sliced roasted turkey
  • 4 large collard leaves
  • 1/4 cup hummus
  • 1/2 cup shredded carrot
  • 1/2 cucumber, julienned
  • 1/4 cup sliced red pepper
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Trim the thick stems from the collard leaves.
  • Spread hummus down the center of each leaf.
  • Layer turkey, carrot, cucumber, and red pepper.
  • Squeeze lemon over the filling.
  • Roll tightly and slice in half.

Ingredients
  • 6 oz grass-fed beef strips
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 1 cup snap peas
  • 1/2 cup sliced carrot
  • 2 Tbsp coconut aminos
  • 1 Tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1 cup cooked brown rice
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat sesame oil in a wok over high heat.
  • Sear beef strips 2 minutes and set aside.
  • Stir-fry broccoli, snap peas, and carrot 4 minutes.
  • Return beef, add ginger and coconut aminos, toss 1 minute.
  • Serve over brown rice.
