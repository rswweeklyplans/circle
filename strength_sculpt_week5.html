RSW — Strength Sculpt

Program: Strength Sculpt — 8-Week Full Body (Strength & Glute Focus) • Week 5 of 8
Goal of Week 5: Push the Ranges: Keep adding load whenever you own the top of the rep range. Your working sets should feel genuinely heavy now.

Week 5 — the middle of the climb. Your loads should be noticeably heavier than Week 1. Keep the jumps small and the form sharp.

This phase: compounds 6–8 reps • accessories 8–10 • isolation 12–15. When all sets hit the top of the rep range with good form, add 2.5–10 lb. Training at home? Bump the reps up (compounds 8–12, accessories 10–15) and lower for 3 slow seconds to make lighter weights feel heavy.

Rest & Recover 🌿

Today is just as important as your training days. Your muscles grow during recovery — not during the workout. Honor this day.

Suggested activities:

  • 30–45 min Zone 2 walk — a pace where you can still hold a conversation
  • Active stretch: hip flexors, hamstrings, chest opener (60 sec each)
  • Foam roll: glutes, quads, upper back (5–10 min)
  • Breathwork or light yoga (10–15 min)
  • Hydrate well and prioritize sleep tonight

Weekly Cardio & Steps

Get in 2–4 Zone 2 walks this week (30–45 minutes at a pace where you can still hold a conversation), plus one optional 10–15 minute interval session. Aim for 8,000–10,000 daily steps — walking supports recovery, regulates hormones, and keeps your metabolism active between sessions.
