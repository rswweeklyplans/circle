Focus: Blood Sugar Balance

This week's meals are built around Blood Sugar Balance. Each plate is designed to be whole-food, home-cook-friendly, and easy to prepare in 40 minutes or less.

Ingredients
  • 3 pasture-raised eggs
  • 4 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 oz goat cheese, crumbled
  • 1 cup arugula
  • 1 Tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Melt butter in a nonstick skillet over medium heat and cook mushrooms until browned, about 6 minutes.
  • Add thyme, salt, and pepper, then slide the mushrooms onto a plate.
  • Whisk the eggs and pour into the pan, swirling to coat.
  • When the edges set, add mushrooms and goat cheese to one half and fold over.
  • Slide onto a plate and top with arugula.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup raw buckwheat groats, soaked overnight
  • 1 1/2 cups unsweetened almond milk
  • 2 Tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 1/3 cup toasted pecans, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup blueberries
  • Pinch of sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Drain and rinse the soaked buckwheat well.
  • Simmer with almond milk, cinnamon, vanilla, and salt for 10 minutes, stirring often.
  • Stir in the flaxseed and cook 2 minutes more until thick.
  • Spoon into bowls and top with pecans and blueberries.

Ingredients
  • 2 slices dense rye bread
  • 1 tin sardines in olive oil, drained
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp red pepper flakes
  • Flaky salt to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toast the rye until firm and crisp.
  • Mash the avocado with lemon juice and a pinch of salt.
  • Spread thickly over each slice.
  • Lay the sardines on top and scatter with red onion and pepper flakes.

Ingredients
  • 2 medium zucchini, grated and squeezed dry
  • 3 eggs
  • 1/2 cup almond flour
  • 1/2 cup crumbled feta
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 2 Tbsp fresh dill, chopped
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Wring the grated zucchini in a clean towel until very dry.
  • Mix with eggs, almond flour, feta, scallions, dill, salt, and pepper.
  • Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium.
  • Drop in 1/4-cup mounds and flatten slightly.
  • Cook 3 to 4 minutes per side until golden and set.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 cups unsweetened coconut yogurt
  • 1/3 cup walnuts, chopped
  • 2 Tbsp cacao nibs
  • 1 Tbsp hemp hearts
  • 1/2 cup raspberries
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp almond butter
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Spoon the coconut yogurt into two bowls.
  • Swirl in the cinnamon and almond butter.
  • Top with walnuts, cacao nibs, and hemp hearts.
  • Add the raspberries last so they hold their shape.

Ingredients
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 cups baby spinach
  • 1 bunch Swiss chard, chopped
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/3 cup crumbled feta
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat olive oil in a skillet and soften the onion for 4 minutes.
  • Add garlic and cumin and cook 1 minute until fragrant.
  • Add chard and spinach in batches, cooking until fully wilted.
  • Make four wells in the greens and crack an egg into each.
  • Cover and cook 6 to 8 minutes until whites set, then top with feta.

Ingredients
  • 4 links chicken sausage, sliced
  • 1 small head cauliflower, riced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 Tbsp parsley, chopped
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Brown the sausage in avocado oil over medium-high, about 5 minutes.
  • Add bell pepper and cook 3 minutes.
  • Stir in riced cauliflower and paprika and cook 6 minutes until tender.
  • Make two wells and crack in the eggs.
  • Cover and cook 4 minutes, then finish with parsley.

Ingredients
  • 1 can chickpeas, rinsed and dried
  • 6 oz halloumi, cubed
  • 2 cups romaine, chopped
  • 1 cup diced cucumber
  • 1/2 cup kalamata olives
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Sear the halloumi cubes in a dry nonstick pan until browned on two sides.
  • Add chickpeas to the pan and toast 4 minutes until they crisp slightly.
  • Whisk olive oil, vinegar, and oregano into a dressing.
  • Toss romaine, cucumber, and olives with the dressing.
  • Top with the warm halloumi and chickpeas.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb cooked shredded chicken
  • 4 cups shredded green cabbage
  • 1 cup shredded carrot
  • 1/2 cup fresh mint leaves
  • 1/3 cup roasted peanuts, chopped
  • 3 Tbsp lime juice
  • 2 Tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 Thai chili, minced
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk lime juice, fish sauce, honey, and chili into a dressing.
  • Toss cabbage and carrot with the dressing and let sit 10 minutes to soften.
  • Fold in the shredded chicken and mint.
  • Top with peanuts just before serving so they stay crunchy.

Ingredients
  • 2 cans black beans, rinsed
  • 2 poblano peppers, roasted and diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Soften the onion and garlic in a pot with a little oil for 5 minutes.
  • Add paprika and cumin and toast 30 seconds.
  • Add beans, poblanos, and broth and simmer 15 minutes.
  • Blend about half the soup for body, then stir it back in.
  • Serve with sour cream and a squeeze of lime.

Ingredients
  • 2 cans wild salmon, drained
  • 1/3 cup Greek yogurt
  • 2 Tbsp whole-grain mustard
  • 2 stalks celery, minced
  • 2 Tbsp capers
  • 3 heads Belgian endive, leaves separated
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • Black pepper to taste

  • Flake the salmon and mix with yogurt, mustard, and lemon juice.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup cooked pearl barley
  • 1 small butternut squash, cubed
  • 8 oz cooked turkey breast, sliced
  • 3 cups baby kale
  • 1/4 cup pumpkin seeds
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Roast the squash at 425F with 1 Tbsp olive oil for 25 minutes until caramelized.
  • Whisk remaining oil with vinegar and Dijon.
  • Toss the warm barley and squash with the kale so it wilts slightly.
  • Add the turkey and dress the bowl.
  • Finish with pumpkin seeds.

Ingredients
  • 1 head cauliflower, riced
  • 1 cup parsley, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup mint, chopped
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, quartered
  • 8 oz halloumi, cubed
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 3 Tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Pulse the cauliflower into rice-size pieces and pat dry.
  • Toss with parsley, mint, tomatoes, olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  • Thread halloumi onto skewers and grill 2 minutes per side.
  • Let the tabbouleh sit 15 minutes so the flavors come together.
  • Serve the skewers over the tabbouleh.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb flank steak, thinly sliced
  • 4 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 head butter lettuce, torn
  • 3 Tbsp tamari
  • 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 Tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toss the steak with 1 Tbsp tamari and let sit 10 minutes.
  • Sear in avocado oil over high heat for 2 minutes, then remove.
  • Add broccoli with a splash of water and cover 3 minutes to steam.
  • Return the beef with garlic, ginger, remaining tamari, and vinegar and toss 1 minute.
  • Finish with sesame oil and spoon over torn lettuce.

Ingredients
  • 4 chicken cutlets
  • 1/3 cup almond flour
  • 3 Tbsp butter
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth
  • 3 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 3 Tbsp capers
  • 8 cups baby spinach
  • 2 cloves garlic, sliced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Season the cutlets and dredge lightly in almond flour.
  • Brown in 2 Tbsp butter over medium-high, 3 minutes per side, then set aside.
  • Add broth, lemon juice, and capers to the pan and simmer 3 minutes.
  • Swirl in the last of the butter and return the chicken to coat.
  • Wilt the spinach with garlic in a second pan and serve alongside.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 lb lamb shoulder, cubed
  • 1 large eggplant, cubed
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cups beef broth
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 cup green olives
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Brown the lamb in olive oil in a heavy pot, working in batches.
  • Add onion and cook 5 minutes, then stir in cinnamon and cumin.
  • Add tomatoes, broth, and eggplant and bring to a simmer.
  • Cover and cook 75 minutes until the lamb is fork tender.
  • Stir in the olives and simmer uncovered 10 minutes to thicken.

Ingredients
  • 4 red snapper fillets (6 oz each)
  • 2 Tbsp blackening seasoning
  • 2 cups julienned jicama
  • 1 cup shredded red cabbage
  • 1/4 cup cilantro, chopped
  • 3 Tbsp lime juice
  • 2 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 1 avocado, sliced
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toss jicama, cabbage, cilantro, and lime juice and chill while you cook.
  • Pat the fillets dry and coat both sides with blackening seasoning.
  • Sear in a very hot cast-iron pan with avocado oil, 3 minutes per side.
  • Plate the fish over the slaw.
  • Top with avocado slices.

Ingredients
  • 6 poblano peppers, halved and seeded
  • 1 lb ground turkey
  • 1 cup cooked quinoa
  • 1 cup fire-roasted tomatoes
  • 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 400F and arrange the poblano halves in a baking dish.
  • Brown the turkey in olive oil with cumin and chili powder, about 7 minutes.
  • Stir in the quinoa and tomatoes and simmer 3 minutes.
  • Fill the peppers and cover with foil.

Ingredients
  • 2 ribeye steaks (12 oz each)
  • 1 lb radishes, halved
  • 3 Tbsp butter
  • 4 cloves garlic, smashed
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • 1 Tbsp beef tallow
  • Coarse salt and cracked pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Roast the radishes at 425F with a little fat and salt for 25 minutes until tender.
  • Salt the steaks heavily and rest at room temperature 30 minutes.
  • Sear in a screaming-hot cast-iron pan with tallow, 3 minutes per side.
  • Add butter, garlic, and rosemary and baste for 90 seconds.
  • Rest 8 minutes before slicing against the grain.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 lb chicken thighs, sliced
  • 3 Tbsp green curry paste
  • 1 can full-fat coconut milk
  • 12 oz green beans, trimmed
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 Tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1/2 cup Thai basil leaves
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Fry the curry paste in a dry pot for 1 minute until fragrant.
  • Add the thick cream from the top of the coconut milk and cook 2 minutes.
  • Add chicken and cook 5 minutes, then pour in the remaining coconut milk.
  • Simmer 10 minutes, then add green beans and pepper for 5 minutes more.
  • Finish with fish sauce, lime juice, and Thai basil.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1/2 lb ground pork
  • 1 egg
  • 1/3 cup almond flour
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan
  • 1 head green cabbage, cut into thick rounds
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1 1/2 cups marinara
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 425F and brush the cabbage rounds with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  • Roast the cabbage on a sheet pan for 20 minutes, flipping once.
  • Mix beef, pork, egg, almond flour, Parmesan, and Italian seasoning and roll into 18 meatballs.
  • Bake the meatballs on a second sheet pan for 18 minutes.
  • Warm the marinara and spoon over the meatballs, served on the cabbage steaks.
