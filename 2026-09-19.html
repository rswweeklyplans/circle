Focus: Mineral Replenish

This week's meals are built around Mineral Replenish. Each plate is designed to be whole-food, home-cook-friendly, and easy to prepare in 40 minutes or less.

Ingredients
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 slices whole-grain sourdough
  • 1/2 cup raw pumpkin seeds
  • 1 cup fresh basil leaves
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 3 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 Tbsp grated Parmesan
  • 1 tsp butter
  • Sea salt and black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toast the pumpkin seeds in a dry skillet for 3 minutes until they start to pop.
  • Blend the seeds with basil, garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, Parmesan, and a pinch of salt into a coarse pesto.
  • Fry the eggs in butter over medium until the whites set and the yolks stay soft.
  • Toast the sourdough and spread each slice with pesto.
  • Top with the eggs and finish with black pepper.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 2 cups whole milk or unsweetened almond milk
  • 1 Tbsp blackstrap molasses
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 cup chopped walnuts
  • 1 Tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 1 ripe banana, sliced
  • Pinch of sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Simmer the oats in milk with a pinch of salt for 7 minutes, stirring often.
  • Stir in the molasses, cinnamon, and flaxseed.
  • Spoon into two bowls.
  • Top with sliced banana and walnuts.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 cups unsweetened almond milk
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 2 Tbsp raw cacao powder
  • 2 Tbsp almond butter
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 1 Tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 pitted Medjool date
  • Pinch of sea salt
  • 1/2 cup ice
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Add the almond milk and spinach to the blender first.
  • Add the banana, cacao, almond butter, chia, date, and salt.
  • Blend on high for 60 seconds until smooth.
  • Add the ice and blend again briefly.
  • Pour and drink right away.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb baby gold potatoes, quartered
  • 4 oz smoked trout, flaked
  • 2 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 yellow onion, diced
  • 2 cups chopped lacinato kale
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 Tbsp fresh dill, chopped
  • 1 tsp whole-grain mustard
  • Sea salt and black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Boil the potatoes in salted water for 8 minutes, then drain well.
  • Crisp the potatoes in olive oil over medium-high for 8 minutes.
  • Add the onion and kale and cook 4 minutes until soft.
  • Stir in the mustard and fold in the trout to warm through.
  • Fry the eggs separately and serve on top with dill and black pepper.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups plain whole-milk Greek yogurt
  • 2 Tbsp tahini
  • 4 pitted Medjool dates, chopped
  • 2 Tbsp sesame seeds
  • 2 Tbsp raw sunflower seeds
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1/4 tsp ground cardamom
  • 1/2 cup pomegranate arils
  • Pinch of flaky sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toast the sesame and sunflower seeds in a dry skillet for 3 minutes until golden.
  • Toss the warm seeds with honey, cardamom, and flaky salt.
  • Divide the yogurt between two bowls and swirl in the tahini.
  • Top with the dates, pomegranate, and sesame crunch.

Ingredients
  • 4 corn tostada shells
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1/4 cup crumbled cotija
  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro
  • 1 lime, cut in wedges

  • Warm the beans in a small pot with cumin, a pinch of salt, and a splash of water, then mash roughly.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup buckwheat flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 ripe banana, mashed
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup whole milk or unsweetened almond milk
  • 1 Tbsp coconut oil, for the pan
  • 2 Tbsp almond butter
  • 1 cup fresh raspberries
  • Pinch of sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk the buckwheat flour, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt together.
  • Stir in the banana, egg, and milk until just combined.
  • Cook 1/4-cup rounds in coconut oil over medium for 2 to 3 minutes per side.
  • Stack and drizzle with almond butter.
  • Top with raspberries.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup quinoa, rinsed
  • 8 oz oyster mushrooms, torn
  • 4 cups baby spinach
  • 2 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbsp coconut aminos
  • 2 Tbsp toasted pumpkin seeds
  • 1 soft-boiled egg per bowl (2 eggs)
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • Sea salt and black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Cook the quinoa in 2 cups salted water for 15 minutes, then fluff.
  • Sear the mushrooms in olive oil over high heat for 5 minutes until golden.
  • Add the garlic and coconut aminos, then wilt in the spinach for 1 minute.
  • Divide the quinoa between two bowls and top with the mushroom mixture.
  • Add a halved soft-boiled egg, pumpkin seeds, and lemon juice.

Ingredients
  • 2 ahi tuna steaks (5 oz each)
  • 8 oz baby potatoes, halved
  • 6 oz green beans, trimmed
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, quartered
  • 4 cups butter lettuce
  • 1/4 cup nicoise or kalamata olives
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 3 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 Tbsp avocado oil
  • Sea salt and black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Boil the potatoes for 10 minutes, adding the green beans for the last 3, then drain.
  • Whisk the olive oil, vinegar, and mustard with salt and pepper.
  • Season the tuna and sear in avocado oil 90 seconds per side, then slice.
  • Arrange the lettuce, potatoes, beans, eggs, olives, and tomatoes on plates.
  • Top with tuna and drizzle with the vinaigrette.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb grass-fed stew beef, cut small
  • 1/2 cup pearl barley
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 6 cups beef bone broth
  • 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 2 cups chopped Swiss chard
  • 1 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • Sea salt and black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Brown the beef in olive oil in a large pot for 5 minutes.
  • Add the onion, carrots, celery, garlic, and thyme and cook 4 minutes.
  • Pour in the broth, tomatoes, and barley and simmer 30 minutes until the barley is tender.
  • Stir in the chard for the last 3 minutes.
  • Season with salt and pepper.

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbsp tahini

  • Cook the soba according to the package, adding the edamame for the last 2 minutes, then rinse under cold water.

  • Coat with the dressing.
  • Top with nori strips and sesame seeds.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup French green lentils

  • 2 oz goat cheese, crumbled
  • 3 cups baby arugula
  • 1 shallot, minced
  • 1 Tbsp sherry vinegar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 Tbsp chopped hazelnuts
  • Sea salt and black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Simmer the lentils in salted water for 20 minutes until tender, then drain.

  • Top with carrots, goat cheese, and hazelnuts.

Ingredients
  • 2 cans (6.5 oz each) chopped clams, with juice
  • 1 can (15 oz) cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 2 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 3 cups chopped escarole or spinach
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 Tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • 2 slices whole-grain sourdough, toasted
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Warm the olive oil and cook the garlic and pepper flakes for 1 minute.
  • Add the broth, beans, and clam juice and simmer 8 minutes.
  • Stir in the greens until wilted.

  • Finish with lemon juice and parsley and serve with toast.

Ingredients
  • 4 halibut fillets (6 oz each)
  • 1 can (15 oz) cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 6 cups baby spinach
  • 3 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 1 Tbsp butter
  • 2 Tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • Sea salt and black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Pat the halibut dry and season with salt and pepper.
  • Sear in 2 Tbsp olive oil over medium-high for 4 minutes per side, then set aside.
  • Add the remaining oil and garlic to the pan, then the beans and broth, and simmer 4 minutes.
  • Wilt in the spinach and stir in the butter, lemon zest, and juice.
  • Serve the fish over the beans and greens with parsley.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 lb grass-fed flank steak
  • 4 medium beets, peeled and cut in 3/4-inch cubes
  • 5 cups baby arugula
  • 1 cup fresh parsley leaves
  • 1/4 cup fresh oregano or cilantro leaves
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1/3 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 2 Tbsp toasted pumpkin seeds
  • Sea salt and black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toss the beets with avocado oil and salt and roast at 425 F for 30 minutes until tender.
  • Finely chop the parsley, oregano, and garlic and stir with the olive oil, vinegar, pepper flakes, and a pinch of salt.
  • Season the steak generously and sear in a hot cast-iron pan 4 to 5 minutes per side for medium-rare.
  • Rest the steak 8 minutes, then slice thinly against the grain.
  • Serve over arugula with the beets, spoon on the chimichurri, and scatter with pumpkin seeds.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 lb wild shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1 large head broccoli, cut in florets
  • 1/2 cup raw cashews
  • 2 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 3 Tbsp coconut aminos
  • 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 Tbsp sesame seeds
  • 2 cups cooked brown rice
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toast the cashews in a dry wok for 2 minutes and set aside.
  • Stir-fry the broccoli in 1 Tbsp avocado oil for 4 minutes with a splash of water.
  • Add the remaining oil, garlic, ginger, and shrimp and cook 3 minutes until pink.
  • Pour in the coconut aminos, vinegar, and sesame oil and toss 1 minute.
  • Add the cashews and sesame seeds and serve over rice.

Ingredients
  • 8 bone-in chicken thighs, skin on
  • 1 1/2 lb baby gold potatoes, halved
  • 2 fennel bulbs, cut in wedges
  • 1/2 cup Castelvetrano olives
  • 3 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, smashed
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 Tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • Sea salt and black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toss the potatoes, fennel, garlic, and olives with 2 Tbsp olive oil and salt on a sheet pan.
  • Rub the chicken with the remaining oil, oregano, salt, and pepper.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 lb ground lamb
  • 2 cups baby spinach, finely chopped
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup almond flour
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 jar (24 oz) no-sugar-added marinara
  • 1 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/4 cup crumbled feta
  • 2 Tbsp fresh mint, chopped
  • 2 cups cooked quinoa
  • Sea salt and black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Mix the lamb, spinach, egg, almond flour, garlic, cumin, salt, and pepper and roll into 16 meatballs.
  • Brown the meatballs in olive oil over medium-high for 5 minutes, turning.
  • Pour in the marinara, cover, and simmer 15 minutes until cooked through.
  • Spoon over quinoa.
  • Top with feta and mint.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 lb dry sea scallops
  • 2 bunches Swiss chard, stems and leaves chopped separately
  • 3 Tbsp butter
  • 1 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 Tbsp toasted pine nuts
  • 1 large sweet potato, cubed and roasted
  • Sea salt and black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Sauté the chard stems with garlic in 1 Tbsp butter for 3 minutes, then add the leaves and wilt 2 minutes.
  • Pat the scallops very dry and season with salt and pepper.
  • Sear in avocado oil over high heat for 2 minutes per side until deeply golden.
  • Add the remaining butter to the pan and cook until it browns and smells nutty, then add the lemon juice.
  • Serve the scallops over the chard and sweet potato with brown butter and pine nuts.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups raw almonds
  • 1 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp fresh rosemary, finely chopped
  • 3/4 tsp sea salt
  • 1/4 tsp garlic powder
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toss the almonds with olive oil, rosemary, salt, and garlic powder.
  • Spread on a parchment-lined sheet pan.
  • Roast at 325 F for 15 minutes, stirring once.
  • Cool completely so they crisp.
  • Store in a jar for up to two weeks.
