Focus: Gut Health & Fiber

This week's meals are built around Gut Health & Fiber. Each plate is designed to be whole-food, home-cook-friendly, and easy to prepare in 40 minutes or less.

Ingredients
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup kimchi, roughly chopped
  • 4 scallions, sliced
  • 1 cup cooked short-grain brown rice
  • 1 Tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 1 tsp gochugaru
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat avocado oil in a skillet and cook the kimchi over medium-high for 4 minutes until it caramelizes at the edges.
  • Add the rice and press it flat to crisp for 3 minutes.
  • Make four wells and crack an egg into each.
  • Cover and cook 5 minutes until the whites set.
  • Finish with sesame oil, scallions, gochugaru, and sesame seeds.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup steel-cut oats
  • 3 cups water
  • 1 cup pitted prunes
  • 1 strip orange peel
  • 3 Tbsp tahini
  • 2 Tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 1/2 tsp cardamom
  • Pinch of sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Simmer the oats in salted water for 25 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  • Meanwhile simmer the prunes with a splash of water, the orange peel, and cardamom for 10 minutes.
  • Stir the flaxseed into the finished oats.
  • Spoon into bowls and top with the stewed prunes.
  • Drizzle tahini generously over each portion.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 cups plain whole-milk kefir
  • 1 slightly green banana
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 2 Tbsp almond butter
  • 1 Tbsp chia seeds
  • 1/2 avocado
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup ice
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Pour the kefir into the blender first so the blades move freely.
  • Add the banana, spinach, avocado, almond butter, chia, and cinnamon.
  • Blend on high for 45 seconds until completely smooth.
  • Add ice and blend again briefly.
  • Drink within 15 minutes while the kefir cultures are lively.

Ingredients
  • 2 sweet potatoes, diced small
  • 1 can black beans, rinsed
  • 1 poblano pepper, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, diced
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1/4 cup cilantro, chopped
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Cook the sweet potato in avocado oil, covered, over medium heat for 10 minutes.
  • Add the onion and poblano and cook 5 minutes uncovered.
  • Stir in the black beans and cumin and heat through.
  • Make four wells and crack in the eggs.
  • Cover and cook 5 minutes, then scatter cilantro over the top.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1 1/4 cups plain kefir
  • 3 Tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 apple, grated with skin on
  • 1/3 cup pistachios, chopped
  • 1 cup raspberries
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 Tbsp maple syrup
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Stir the oats, kefir, chia, grated apple, vanilla, and maple syrup together.
  • Cover and refrigerate at least 6 hours or overnight.
  • Stir well and loosen with a splash of kefir if it is too thick.
  • Divide into jars and top with raspberries and pistachios.

Ingredients
  • 4 cups dashi or vegetable broth
  • 3 Tbsp white miso paste
  • 6 oz shiitake mushrooms, sliced
  • 4 soft-boiled eggs
  • 2 cups baby bok choy, chopped
  • 1 Tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1 sheet nori, torn
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Simmer the broth with ginger and shiitakes for 8 minutes.
  • Add the bok choy and cook 2 minutes until just tender.
  • Take the pot off the heat and whisk the miso into a ladle of broth before stirring it back in.
  • Ladle into bowls and add a halved soft-boiled egg to each.
  • Top with scallions and torn nori.

Ingredients
  • 4 slices dense whole-grain rye bread
  • 1 cup raw sauerkraut, drained
  • 2 ripe avocados
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 Tbsp pumpkin seeds
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds
  • Flaky salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toast the rye until firm.
  • Mash the avocado with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  • Spread over each slice and press a hollow into the center.
  • Pile the sauerkraut on top.
  • Finish with pumpkin seeds, caraway, and flaky salt.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 cups cooked farro
  • 3 beets, roasted and cubed
  • 1 cup labneh
  • 1/3 cup walnuts, toasted
  • 2 cups arugula
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp sherry vinegar
  • 2 Tbsp fresh dill
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Roast the beets at 400F for 45 minutes until a knife slides in easily, then peel and cube.
  • Whisk the olive oil and sherry vinegar with salt and pepper.
  • Toss the farro, beets, and arugula with the dressing.
  • Spread the labneh across the base of a platter and pile the salad on top.
  • Scatter walnuts and dill over everything.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb dried green split peas, rinsed
  • 1 smoked ham hock
  • 8 cups chicken broth
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Soften the onion, carrot, celery, and garlic in a heavy pot for 6 minutes.
  • Add the split peas, ham hock, broth, and bay leaves.
  • Simmer partly covered for 70 minutes until the peas break down.
  • Pull the hock out, shred the meat, and return it to the pot.
  • Season well and thin with broth if it gets too thick.

Ingredients
  • 8 oz tempeh, cubed
  • 4 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 1/2 cups cooked barley
  • 1/4 cup tahini
  • 3 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 3 Tbsp tamari
  • 2 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 1 clove garlic, grated
  • 2 Tbsp sunflower seeds
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Steam the tempeh 10 minutes to mellow it, then toss with tamari.
  • Roast the tempeh and broccoli at 425F with avocado oil for 20 minutes.
  • Whisk the tahini, lemon juice, garlic, and enough water to make a pourable sauce.
  • Build bowls with barley, broccoli, and tempeh.
  • Spoon the tahini sauce over and top with sunflower seeds.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 cups red lentils, rinsed
  • 4 carrots, chopped
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 5 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 can full-fat coconut milk
  • 2 Tbsp curry powder
  • 1 Tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 Tbsp coconut oil
  • 1 lime, juiced
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Melt the coconut oil and cook the onion and carrot for 7 minutes.
  • Add the ginger and curry powder and toast 1 minute.
  • Add the lentils and broth and simmer 20 minutes until everything is soft.
  • Blend until smooth, then stir in the coconut milk.
  • Finish with lime juice and season to taste.

Ingredients
  • 8 oz smoked mackerel, flaked
  • 1 1/2 cups cooked freekeh
  • 1 fennel bulb, shaved thin
  • 1 green apple, julienned
  • 1/3 cup parsley, chopped
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 3 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 Tbsp whole-grain mustard
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Shave the fennel as thin as you can and soak it in ice water for 10 minutes to crisp.
  • Whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, and mustard together.
  • Toss the freekeh, drained fennel, apple, and parsley with the dressing.
  • Fold in the flaked mackerel at the very end so it stays in large pieces.

Ingredients
  • 1 can chickpeas, rinsed
  • 1 jar marinated artichoke hearts, halved
  • 1 cup roasted red peppers, sliced
  • 1 cup tzatziki
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup pitted olives
  • 2 whole-wheat pitas, warmed
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toss the chickpeas with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  • Arrange the chickpeas, artichokes, peppers, tomatoes, and olives on a wide platter.
  • Spoon the tzatziki into the middle.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb cooked shredded chicken
  • 1 1/2 cups cooked brown rice
  • 1 cup kimchi
  • 2 cups shredded cabbage
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 2 Tbsp gochujang
  • 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 Tbsp honey
  • 1 Tbsp sesame oil
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk the gochujang, rice vinegar, honey, and sesame oil into a sauce.

Ingredients
  • 4 Italian sausage links, sliced
  • 2 cans cannellini beans, rinsed
  • 1 large head escarole, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 3 cups chicken broth
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp red pepper flakes
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Brown the sausage in olive oil in a wide pot, about 6 minutes.
  • Add the garlic and red pepper flakes and cook 1 minute.
  • Add the beans and broth and simmer 10 minutes, mashing a few beans to thicken.
  • Fold in the escarole in batches and cook 5 minutes until tender.
  • Finish with Parmesan and a drizzle of olive oil.

Ingredients
  • 1 head cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 1 can chickpeas, rinsed and dried
  • 1 1/2 cups cooked bulgur
  • 1/2 cup tahini sauce
  • 1 cup pickled red onion
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp shawarma spice blend
  • 1/2 cup parsley, chopped
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 425F.
  • Toss the cauliflower and chickpeas with olive oil and shawarma spice.
  • Roast on a sheet pan for 30 minutes, stirring once, until deeply browned.
  • Divide the bulgur among bowls and top with the roasted vegetables.
  • Add pickled onion, tahini sauce, and parsley.

Ingredients
  • 4 salmon fillets (6 oz each)
  • 3 leeks, halved and cleaned
  • 1 1/2 cups cooked green lentils
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 3 Tbsp butter
  • 2 Tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 Tbsp fresh tarragon
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Melt 2 Tbsp butter in an oven-safe skillet and lay the leeks cut-side down.
  • Brown for 5 minutes, add the broth, and braise covered for 12 minutes.
  • Stir the lentils and Dijon into the pan.
  • Season the salmon and set it on top of the leeks and lentils.
  • Roast at 400F for 12 minutes, then finish with tarragon and the last of the butter.

Ingredients
  • 1 large head savoy cabbage
  • 1 lb ground turkey
  • 1 cup cooked barley
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp dried marjoram
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Boil the whole cabbage 6 minutes, then peel off 12 large leaves and trim the thick ribs.
  • Mix the turkey, barley, grated onion, garlic, marjoram, salt, and pepper.
  • Roll about 1/4 cup of filling into each leaf, tucking in the sides.
  • Nestle the rolls seam-side down in a baking dish and cover with the tomatoes.
  • Bake covered at 350F for 60 minutes.

Ingredients
  • 1 whole chicken (4 lb)
  • 3 Tbsp white miso paste
  • 4 Tbsp softened butter
  • 1 lb parsnips, cut into batons
  • 1 lb carrots, cut into batons
  • 1 fennel bulb, wedged
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, halved
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Mash the miso into the softened butter and rub it under and over the chicken skin.
  • Put the lemon halves in the cavity and truss the legs.
  • Toss the parsnips, carrots, and fennel with olive oil in a roasting pan and set the chicken on top.
  • Roast at 425F for 20 minutes, then reduce to 375F and cook 55 minutes more to 165F.
  • Rest 15 minutes before carving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup split mung dal, rinsed
  • 3/4 cup basmati rice
  • 6 cups water
  • 1 delicata squash, sliced into half moons
  • 2 Tbsp ghee
  • 1 Tbsp cumin seeds
  • 1 Tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1/2 cup cilantro, chopped
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Roast the squash at 425F with a little ghee for 25 minutes.
  • Bloom the cumin seeds in the remaining ghee until they pop, about 40 seconds.
  • Add the ginger and turmeric, then the dal, rice, and water.
  • Simmer partly covered for 35 minutes until soft and porridge-like.
  • Top each bowl with roasted squash and cilantro.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 lb shrimp, peeled
  • 1 lb okra, sliced
  • 1/3 cup avocado oil
  • 1/3 cup whole-wheat flour
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 6 cups seafood stock
  • 2 cups cooked brown rice
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk the oil and flour in a heavy pot over medium heat and stir constantly for 15 minutes until the roux turns deep brown.
  • Add the onion, pepper, and celery and cook 6 minutes.
  • Pour in the stock slowly, whisking, then add the okra.
  • Simmer 30 minutes until thickened.
  • Add the shrimp and cook 4 minutes, then serve over brown rice.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups unsweetened coconut yogurt
  • 3 stalks rhubarb, chopped
  • 2 Tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 strip orange peel
  • 1/4 cup toasted almonds, chopped
  • 1 Tbsp ground flaxseed
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Simmer the rhubarb with maple syrup, orange peel, and a splash of water for 8 minutes until it collapses.

Ingredients
  • 1 can chickpeas, rinsed
  • 1 medium beet, roasted and peeled
  • 1/4 cup tahini
  • 3 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • Seeded whole-grain crackers to serve
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Blend the chickpeas, beet, tahini, lemon juice, garlic, and cumin until very smooth.
  • Stream in the olive oil with the motor running.
  • Thin with cold water a tablespoon at a time until it is light and scoopable.
  • Season with salt and serve with seeded crackers.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup raw almonds
  • 1 cup raw pumpkin seeds
  • 2 Tbsp white miso paste
  • 1 Tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk the miso, maple syrup, oil, and vinegar into a thin glaze.
  • Toss the almonds and pumpkin seeds until evenly coated.
  • Spread on a parchment-lined sheet pan.
  • Roast at 300F for 22 minutes, stirring twice.
  • Cool completely on the pan so they crisp up.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup thick plain kefir
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 2 Tbsp fresh dill, chopped
  • 2 Tbsp chives, minced
  • 1 clove garlic, grated
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 4 cups mixed raw vegetables
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk the kefir and sour cream together until thick and smooth.
  • Stir in the dill, chives, garlic, and lemon juice.
  • Season well and chill 30 minutes so the herbs infuse.
  • Serve with cut raw vegetables for dipping.

Ingredients
  • 8 oz 85% dark chocolate
  • 1/3 cup toasted hazelnuts, chopped
  • 1/4 cup dried figs, chopped
  • 2 Tbsp chia seeds
  • 2 Tbsp puffed quinoa
  • 1/2 tsp flaky salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Melt the chocolate slowly over a pot of barely simmering water.
  • Pour onto a parchment-lined sheet pan and spread to about 1/4 inch thick.
  • Scatter the hazelnuts, figs, chia, and puffed quinoa over the surface.
  • Sprinkle with flaky salt and chill 30 minutes until firm.
  • Break into shards and store in the fridge.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups mixed vegetables (carrot, radish, cauliflower)
  • 1 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 Tbsp honey
  • 1 Tbsp sea salt
  • 1 tsp mustard seed
  • 1 tsp dill seed
  • 5 oz aged Gouda, sliced
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Pack the cut vegetables tightly into a clean jar with the mustard and dill seeds.
  • Bring the vinegar, water, honey, and salt to a boil.
  • Pour the hot brine over the vegetables to cover.
  • Cool to room temperature, then refrigerate at least 4 hours.
  • Serve the pickles alongside sliced Gouda.

Ingredients
  • 3 firm apples, sliced paper thin
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup almond butter
  • 1/3 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 Tbsp maple syrup
  • Pinch of sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 225F and line two sheet pans with parchment.
  • Lay the apple slices flat without overlapping and dust with cinnamon.
  • Bake 90 minutes, flipping halfway, until dry and crisp at the edges.
  • Whisk the almond butter, yogurt, maple syrup, and salt into a dip.
  • Cool the chips completely before serving so they stay crisp.
