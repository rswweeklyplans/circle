Focus: Anti-Inflammatory

This week's meals are built around Anti-Inflammatory. Each plate is designed to be whole-food, home-cook-friendly, and easy to prepare in 40 minutes or less.

Ingredients
  • 6 eggs
  • 1 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3 cups baby spinach
  • 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 cup crumbled feta
  • 2 Tbsp chopped fresh parsley
  • 1/2 avocado, sliced
  • Pinch of sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk the eggs with turmeric, black pepper, and salt until evenly golden.
  • Warm the olive oil in a nonstick skillet over medium and wilt the spinach for 1 minute.
  • Pour in the eggs and stir slowly with a spatula until soft curds form, about 3 minutes.
  • Fold in the feta and remove from heat while the eggs are still glossy.
  • Top with parsley and sliced avocado.

Ingredients
  • 1/4 cup chia seeds
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 tsp freshly grated ginger
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 Tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 cup fresh blueberries
  • 2 Tbsp chopped walnuts
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk the almond milk, yogurt, ginger, vanilla, and maple syrup together.
  • Stir in the chia seeds and let sit 5 minutes, then whisk again to break up clumps.
  • Refrigerate at least 4 hours or overnight until thick.
  • Divide between two jars and top with blueberries.
  • Finish with chopped walnuts just before eating.

Ingredients
  • 1 large sweet potato, sliced lengthwise 1/4 inch thick
  • 4 oz smoked wild salmon
  • 1/3 cup whipped cottage cheese
  • 1 Tbsp fresh dill, chopped
  • 1 Tbsp capers
  • 1/4 small red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 lemon, cut in wedges
  • 1 tsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • Black pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Brush the sweet potato slices with olive oil and toast in a toaster or 400 F oven for 15 minutes, flipping once, until tender.
  • Spread each slice with whipped cottage cheese.
  • Layer on the smoked salmon and red onion.
  • Scatter capers and dill over the top.
  • Finish with a squeeze of lemon and black pepper.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup frozen tart cherries
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 1/2 frozen banana
  • 1 Tbsp raw cacao powder
  • 2 Tbsp hemp hearts
  • 1 Tbsp almond butter
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 handful baby kale
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Add the almond milk and kale to the blender first.
  • Add the cherries, banana, cacao, hemp hearts, almond butter, and cinnamon.
  • Blend on high for 60 seconds until completely smooth.
  • Add a splash more milk if it is too thick.
  • Pour into a glass and drink right away.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 2 cups water
  • 2 Tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 2 ripe pears, diced
  • 1 tsp coconut oil
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp ground ginger
  • 1/4 cup chopped walnuts
  • 1 Tbsp maple syrup
  • Pinch of sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Simmer the oats in salted water over medium-low for 8 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  • Meanwhile melt the coconut oil in a small skillet and cook the pears with cinnamon and ginger for 5 minutes until soft.
  • Stir the flaxseed into the finished oats.
  • Spoon the oats into bowls and top with the spiced pears.
  • Finish with walnuts and a drizzle of maple syrup.

Ingredients
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 can (4 oz) sardines in olive oil, drained
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1/4 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/4 cup kalamata olives, halved
  • 2 Tbsp fresh basil, torn
  • Sea salt and black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Warm the olive oil in a skillet over medium and cook the garlic for 1 minute.
  • Add the tomatoes and paprika and cook 4 minutes until they blister and soften.
  • Nestle in the sardines and olives to warm through.
  • Crack the eggs over the top, cover, and cook 5 minutes until the whites are set.
  • Season and finish with torn basil.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup unsweetened coconut milk beverage
  • 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 tsp matcha powder
  • 1 Tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 Tbsp honey
  • 1/4 cup shelled pistachios, chopped
  • 1/2 cup sliced strawberries

  • Whisk the matcha into the coconut milk until no lumps remain.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb boneless chicken thighs
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 cup quinoa, rinsed
  • 2 cups baby arugula
  • 1 cup cucumber, diced
  • 1/4 cup fresh mint, chopped
  • 3 Tbsp tahini
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 Tbsp water
  • Sea salt and black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Cook the quinoa in 2 cups salted water for 15 minutes, then fluff and cool.
  • Rub the chicken with turmeric, cumin, olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  • Sear the chicken in a hot skillet 5 to 6 minutes per side until cooked through, then slice.
  • Whisk the tahini, lemon juice, and water into a smooth dressing.
  • Toss the quinoa with arugula, cucumber, and mint, top with chicken, and drizzle with the dressing.

Ingredients
  • 2 cans (5 oz each) wild salmon, drained
  • 1 ripe avocado, mashed
  • 2 Tbsp plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 Tbsp fresh dill, chopped
  • 2 celery stalks, finely diced
  • 2 Tbsp toasted pumpkin seeds
  • 4 cups mixed greens
  • 1 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • Sea salt and black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Flake the salmon into a bowl and remove any large bones.
  • Fold in the mashed avocado, yogurt, lemon juice, dill, and celery.
  • Season with salt and pepper.
  • Dress the greens with olive oil and a pinch of salt.
  • Pile the salmon salad over the greens and scatter with pumpkin seeds.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 lb carrots, chopped
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 2 Tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 Tbsp coconut oil
  • 4 cups low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 1/2 cup full-fat coconut milk
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • Fresh cilantro for serving
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Melt the coconut oil in a pot and soften the onion for 5 minutes.
  • Add the ginger, garlic, turmeric, and coriander and stir for 1 minute until fragrant.
  • Add the carrots, lentils, and broth and simmer 20 minutes until the lentils collapse.
  • Blend until smooth, then stir in the coconut milk and lime juice.
  • Serve topped with cilantro.

Ingredients
  • 2 cans (4 oz each) mackerel in olive oil, drained
  • 1 cup cooked farro
  • 2 medium beets, roasted and cubed
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 1/4 cup walnuts, toasted
  • 2 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp honey
  • Sea salt and black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk the olive oil, vinegar, mustard, honey, salt, and pepper into a dressing.
  • Toss the farro with half the dressing.
  • Divide the spinach and farro between two bowls.
  • Top with beets, flaked mackerel, and walnuts.
  • Drizzle the remaining dressing over everything.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb ground chicken
  • 1 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 Tbsp coconut aminos
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 cup shredded carrots
  • 1 head butter lettuce, leaves separated
  • 3 Tbsp natural peanut butter
  • 1 Tbsp lime juice
  • 2 Tbsp warm water
  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the avocado oil in a skillet and brown the chicken over medium-high for 6 minutes, breaking it up.
  • Add the garlic, ginger, bell pepper, and coconut aminos and cook 3 more minutes.
  • Whisk the peanut butter, lime juice, and warm water into a pourable sauce.
  • Spoon the chicken into lettuce leaves and top with shredded carrots.
  • Drizzle with peanut sauce and garnish with cilantro.

Ingredients
  • 1 large head broccoli, cut in florets
  • 1 can (15 oz) cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 3 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/4 cup kalamata olives, chopped
  • 1 Tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1/4 cup shaved Parmesan
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • Sea salt and black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toss the broccoli with 1 Tbsp olive oil and salt and roast at 425 F for 18 minutes until charred at the edges.
  • Whisk the remaining olive oil with the olives, vinegar, garlic, and pepper flakes.
  • Combine the warm broccoli with the beans in a large bowl.
  • Toss with the olive vinaigrette and lemon zest.
  • Finish with shaved Parmesan and black pepper.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb wild shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 1/2 tsp chili powder
  • 1 ripe mango, diced
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 jalapeno, minced
  • 4 cups chopped romaine
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 2 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
  • Sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toss the shrimp with avocado oil, chili powder, and salt.

  • Whisk the lime juice and olive oil with a pinch of salt.

  • Top with the warm shrimp and cilantro.

Ingredients
  • 4 wild salmon fillets (6 oz each)
  • 2 Tbsp white miso paste
  • 1 Tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 Tbsp honey
  • 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 Tbsp coconut aminos
  • 4 baby bok choy, halved
  • 1 Tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds
  • 2 scallions, sliced
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk the miso, ginger, honey, rice vinegar, and coconut aminos into a glaze.
  • Brush the salmon with half the glaze and roast at 400 F for 12 minutes.
  • Sear the bok choy cut side down in sesame oil over medium-high for 3 minutes, then flip and cook 2 more.
  • Brush the salmon with the remaining glaze during the final 2 minutes.
  • Serve over the bok choy with sesame seeds and scallions.

Ingredients
  • 8 bone-in chicken thighs, skin on
  • 1 head cauliflower, cut in florets
  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 3 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 lemon, halved
  • 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 Tbsp fresh cilantro
  • Sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Rub the chicken with 1 Tbsp olive oil, turmeric, cumin, paprika, pepper, and salt.
  • Toss the cauliflower and chickpeas with the remaining oil and a pinch of salt on a sheet pan.
  • Nestle the chicken skin side up among the vegetables and roast at 425 F for 35 minutes.
  • Squeeze the lemon over the pan as it comes out of the oven.
  • Serve with a spoonful of Greek yogurt and cilantro.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb grass-fed flank steak, thinly sliced
  • 1 bunch lacinato kale, stemmed and chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 2 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 3 Tbsp coconut aminos
  • 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tsp arrowroot starch
  • 2 cups cooked brown rice
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk the coconut aminos, vinegar, sesame oil, and arrowroot into a sauce.
  • Sear the steak in 1 Tbsp avocado oil over high heat for 2 minutes, then set aside.
  • Add the remaining oil, garlic, ginger, and bell pepper and stir-fry 2 minutes.
  • Add the kale and cook 3 minutes until wilted.
  • Return the beef, pour in the sauce, and toss 1 minute until glossy. Serve over rice.

Ingredients
  • 4 wild cod fillets (6 oz each)
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup Castelvetrano olives, pitted
  • 1 shallot, thinly sliced
  • 3 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, smashed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 lb green beans, trimmed
  • Sea salt and black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toss the tomatoes, olives, shallot, garlic, green beans, and 2 Tbsp olive oil on a sheet pan and roast at 425 F for 12 minutes.
  • Season the cod with oregano, pepper flakes, salt, and pepper.

  • Roast 12 more minutes until the fish flakes.
  • Scatter with parsley before serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 lb boneless chicken thighs, cubed
  • 1 large sweet potato, cubed
  • 1 can (14 oz) full-fat coconut milk
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 2 Tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbsp ground turmeric
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 Tbsp coconut oil
  • 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • 3 cups baby spinach
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 2 cups cooked basmati rice
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Melt the coconut oil in a large pot and brown the chicken 5 minutes, then remove.
  • Soften the onion 4 minutes, then add the ginger, garlic, turmeric, and curry powder for 1 minute.
  • Add the sweet potato, coconut milk, and broth and simmer 15 minutes until tender.
  • Return the chicken and cook 5 minutes, then stir in the spinach until wilted.
  • Finish with lime juice and serve over rice.

Ingredients
  • 8 lamb loin chops
  • 2 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 3 cups frozen peas
  • 1/4 cup fresh mint leaves
  • 2 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 bunch asparagus, trimmed
  • Sea salt and black pepper
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Rub the lamb with 2 Tbsp olive oil, rosemary, garlic, lemon zest, salt, and pepper and rest 10 minutes.
  • Boil the peas 3 minutes, drain, and mash with mint, 2 Tbsp olive oil, lemon juice, and salt.

  • Roast the asparagus at 425 F for 8 minutes with a drizzle of oil and salt.
  • Serve the chops over the pea mash with asparagus alongside.

Ingredients
  • 4 large bell peppers, halved and seeded
  • 1 cup cooked green lentils
  • 1/2 cup walnuts, finely chopped
  • 1 cup cooked quinoa
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (14 oz) fire-roasted diced tomatoes
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 Tbsp fresh dill and parsley, chopped

Ingredients
  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained, rinsed, and patted dry
  • 1 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toss the dry chickpeas with olive oil and all the spices.
  • Spread on a parchment-lined sheet pan in a single layer.
  • Roast at 400 F for 25 minutes, shaking the pan halfway.
  • Cool 10 minutes so they crisp up.
  • Store in an open container for up to 3 days.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup pitted Medjool dates
  • 1 cup raw cashews
  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 2 tsp matcha powder
  • 2 Tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of sea salt
  • 2 Tbsp unsweetened shredded coconut
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Pulse the cashews and oats in a food processor until coarse.
  • Add the dates, matcha, flaxseed, vanilla, and salt and process until the mixture holds together.
  • Roll into 12 balls.
  • Roll each in shredded coconut.
  • Chill 30 minutes and store in the fridge for up to a week.

  • 1 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • 1 Tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
  • Flaky sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe

Ingredients
  • 1 lb fresh strawberries
  • 4 oz dark chocolate (85 percent), chopped
  • 1 tsp coconut oil
  • 1/4 cup shelled pistachios, finely chopped
  • Pinch of flaky sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Melt the chocolate and coconut oil in a double boiler, stirring until smooth.
  • Dip each strawberry two-thirds of the way in and let the excess drip off.
  • Set on parchment and sprinkle with pistachios and flaky salt.
  • Chill 20 minutes until the chocolate sets.
  • Keep refrigerated and eat within 2 days.
