Focus: Recovery & Strength

This week's meals are built around Recovery & Strength. Each plate is designed to be whole-food, home-cook-friendly, and easy to prepare in 40 minutes or less.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb ground turkey
  • 1 tsp fennel seed
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, diced small
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 4 pasture-raised eggs
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat 1 Tbsp olive oil in a large skillet and cook the sweet potatoes 10 to 12 minutes until tender and browned, then set aside.
  • Add the remaining oil, the turkey, fennel, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper and cook 6 to 8 minutes, breaking it up.
  • Stir in the bell pepper and sweet potatoes and cook 3 minutes.
  • Make 4 wells and crack an egg into each, cover, and cook 5 minutes until set.
  • Top with scallions and serve.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 1/2 cup whole-milk Greek yogurt
  • 2 scoops unflavored collagen peptides
  • 2 Tbsp chia seeds
  • 1/2 cup frozen tart cherries, thawed
  • 2 Tbsp almond butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Stir the oats, almond milk, yogurt, collagen, chia, vanilla, and salt in a jar.
  • Fold in the cherries.
  • Refrigerate overnight or at least 4 hours.
  • Top with almond butter before serving.

Ingredients
  • 12 oz fresh chicken chorizo, casings removed
  • 6 pasture-raised eggs, beaten
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 cup black beans, rinsed
  • 1 cup baby spinach
  • 4 large whole-grain or sprouted tortillas
  • 1/2 cup shredded Monterey Jack
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • Salsa for serving
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Cook the chorizo in a skillet over medium heat 6 to 7 minutes until browned, then transfer to a plate.
  • Add the olive oil and scramble the eggs with salt and pepper until just set.
  • Stir in the chorizo, black beans, and spinach until the spinach wilts.
  • Warm the tortillas and fill each with the egg mixture, cheese, and avocado.
  • Roll tightly and serve with salsa.

Ingredients
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 2 Tbsp natural peanut butter
  • 1/4 cup rolled oats
  • 1 scoop vanilla whey or plant protein
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 1/2 cup whole-milk Greek yogurt
  • 1 Tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup ice
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Add the almond milk and oats to a blender and let sit 2 minutes.
  • Add the banana, peanut butter, protein, yogurt, flaxseed, and cinnamon.
  • Blend until smooth, adding ice for a thicker texture.
  • Pour into 2 glasses and serve.

Ingredients
  • 6 pasture-raised eggs
  • 2 Tbsp whole-milk Greek yogurt
  • 1 Tbsp butter
  • 4 oz smoked salmon, torn
  • 2 Tbsp fresh dill, chopped
  • 2 Tbsp chives, chopped
  • 4 slices sourdough, toasted
  • 1/2 lemon
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk the eggs with the yogurt, salt, and pepper.
  • Melt the butter in a nonstick skillet over medium-low heat and add the eggs.
  • Stir slowly 3 to 4 minutes until soft curds form.
  • Fold in the salmon, dill, and chives and remove from the heat.
  • Spoon over the sourdough and finish with a squeeze of lemon.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups whole-milk cottage cheese
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1/4 cup chopped almonds
  • 2 Tbsp pumpkin seeds
  • 2 Tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 Tbsp coconut oil, melted
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 1/2 cups mixed berries
  • 2 Tbsp hemp hearts
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the oven to 350F.
  • Toss the oats, almonds, pumpkin seeds, maple syrup, coconut oil, and cinnamon on a sheet pan.
  • Bake 15 to 18 minutes, stirring once, until golden, then cool.
  • Divide the cottage cheese between 2 bowls.
  • Top with berries, granola clusters, and hemp hearts.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup whole-milk Greek yogurt
  • 2 pasture-raised eggs
  • 1 cup oat flour
  • 1 scoop vanilla protein powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of sea salt
  • 1 Tbsp butter
  • 1 1/2 cups blueberries
  • 1 Tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Simmer the blueberries, maple syrup, and lemon juice in a small pan 6 to 8 minutes until jammy.
  • Whisk the yogurt, eggs, and vanilla, then stir in the oat flour, protein, baking powder, and salt.
  • Melt the butter on a griddle over medium heat.
  • Cook 1/4 cup scoops of batter 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden.
  • Serve topped with the blueberry compote.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb flank or sirloin steak
  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, cubed
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 6 cups mixed greens
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the oven to 425F and roast the sweet potatoes with 2 Tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper 20 minutes.
  • Stir the parsley, cilantro, garlic, 1/4 cup olive oil, vinegar, pepper flakes, and salt into a chimichurri.
  • Season the steak and grill or sear 4 to 5 minutes per side for medium-rare, then rest 5 minutes and slice thin.
  • Arrange the greens and tomatoes on plates with the sweet potatoes.
  • Top with the sliced steak and spoon the chimichurri over.

Ingredients
  • 2 cans (5 oz each) wild tuna, drained

  • 1/4 cup whole-milk Greek yogurt

  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 celery stalk, minced
  • 2 Tbsp red onion, minced
  • 1 Tbsp capers, chopped

  • Salt and pepper to taste

  • Flake the tuna into a bowl with the quinoa.

  • Fill the avocado halves generously with the tuna mixture and serve.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb grass-fed stew beef, cut into 1/2 inch pieces
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 8 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 6 cups beef bone broth
  • 3/4 cup quick-cooking barley
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 2 cups chopped kale
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the olive oil in a large pot and brown the beef 5 minutes, then remove.
  • Cook the onion, carrots, celery, and mushrooms 6 minutes, then add the garlic and thyme for 1 minute.
  • Return the beef, add the broth and barley, and simmer 20 minutes until the barley is tender.
  • Stir in the kale and cook 3 minutes.
  • Season with salt and pepper and serve.

Ingredients

Ingredients
  • 1 lb ground chicken
  • 1/4 cup almond flour
  • 1 pasture-raised egg
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 4 whole-grain pitas
  • 1 cup whole-milk Greek yogurt
  • 1/2 cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
  • 1 Tbsp fresh dill, chopped
  • 2 cups shredded romaine
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Mix the chicken, almond flour, egg, garlic, oregano, cumin, salt, and pepper and roll into 16 small meatballs.
  • Heat the olive oil in a skillet and cook the meatballs 10 to 12 minutes, turning, until browned and cooked through.
  • Stir the yogurt with the cucumber, dill, and a pinch of salt for tzatziki.
  • Warm the pitas and fill with romaine, tomato, and meatballs.
  • Top with tzatziki and serve.

Ingredients
  • 4 wild salmon fillets (5 oz each)
  • 3 Tbsp coconut aminos
  • 1 Tbsp honey
  • 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 2 cups cooked brown rice
  • 1 cup shelled edamame, steamed
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 1 cup shredded carrot
  • 1 Tbsp sesame seeds
  • 2 scallions, sliced
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk the coconut aminos, honey, rice vinegar, ginger, and garlic.
  • Heat the avocado oil in a skillet and sear the salmon 4 minutes skin-side down.
  • Flip, pour in the sauce, and cook 3 to 4 minutes, spooning the glaze over the fish.
  • Divide the rice between 4 bowls with the edamame, cucumber, and carrot.
  • Top with the salmon, extra glaze, sesame seeds, and scallions.

Ingredients
  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 1/2 lb baby potatoes
  • 1 lb green beans, trimmed
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Boil the potatoes 12 minutes until just tender, then drain and smash lightly.
  • Heat the oven to 425F and rub the chicken with 1 Tbsp olive oil, garlic, rosemary, thyme, salt, and pepper.
  • Place the chicken and potatoes on a sheet pan, drizzle the potatoes with 1 Tbsp olive oil, and roast 15 minutes.
  • Add the green beans tossed with the remaining oil and roast 8 to 10 more minutes until the chicken reaches 165F.
  • Serve with lemon wedges.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 lb ground bison
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 yellow onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 Tbsp butter
  • 2 large sweet potatoes, cut into wedges
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 4 whole-grain buns or lettuce wraps
  • 4 slices sharp cheddar, optional
  • Dijon mustard for serving
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the oven to 425F and roast the sweet potato wedges with olive oil, salt, and pepper 25 minutes, flipping once.
  • Cook the onions in butter over medium-low heat 15 to 18 minutes, stirring, until deeply golden.
  • Mix the bison with garlic powder, paprika, Worcestershire, salt, and pepper and form 4 patties.
  • Sear the patties 4 minutes per side, adding cheese for the last minute if using.
  • Serve on buns or in lettuce with the onions, Dijon, and sweet potato wedges.

Ingredients
  • 4 wild cod fillets (6 oz each)
  • 2 Tbsp white miso
  • 1 Tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 Tbsp honey
  • 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 Tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 4 heads baby bok choy, halved
  • 1 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 2 cups cooked brown rice
  • 1 Tbsp sesame seeds
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • Salt to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the oven to 425F.
  • Whisk the miso, ginger, honey, rice vinegar, and sesame oil and brush over the cod.
  • Toss the bok choy with avocado oil and salt on a sheet pan and place the cod alongside.
  • Roast 12 to 14 minutes until the cod flakes and the bok choy is charred at the edges.
  • Serve over brown rice with sesame seeds and scallions.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 lb wild shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 3 Tbsp butter
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 bunch asparagus, cut into 2 inch pieces
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • Zest and juice of 1 lemon
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • 3 cups cooked quinoa
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the olive oil and 1 Tbsp butter in a large skillet and cook the asparagus 4 minutes until crisp-tender, then remove.
  • Add the remaining butter, garlic, and pepper flakes and cook 1 minute.
  • Add the shrimp with salt and pepper and cook 2 minutes per side until pink.

Ingredients
  • 4 bone-in pork chops (1 inch thick)
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried sage
  • 1 lb Brussels sprouts, halved
  • 1 shallot, minced
  • 3/4 cup apple cider
  • 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 Tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 Tbsp butter
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the oven to 425F and roast the Brussels sprouts with 1 Tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper 20 to 22 minutes.
  • Season the chops with sage, salt, and pepper and sear in the remaining oil 4 to 5 minutes per side until 145F, then rest on a plate.
  • Cook the shallot in the same skillet 1 minute.
  • Add the cider, broth, and Dijon and simmer 4 minutes until reduced by half, then swirl in the butter.
  • Serve the chops with the pan sauce and Brussels sprouts.
