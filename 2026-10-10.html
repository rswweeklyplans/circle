Focus: Micronutrient Density

This week's meals are built around Micronutrient Density. Each plate is designed to be whole-food, home-cook-friendly, and easy to prepare in 40 minutes or less.

Ingredients
  • 3 cups butternut squash, peeled and diced small
  • 1 small red onion, diced
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp fresh sage, chopped
  • 4 pasture-raised eggs
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 1/4 tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  • Add the squash and onion with a pinch of salt and cook 12 to 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned and tender.
  • Stir in the sage, paprika, and spinach and cook until the spinach wilts.
  • Make 4 wells in the hash and crack an egg into each.
  • Cover and cook 4 to 5 minutes until the whites are set and the yolks are still soft.
  • Season with salt and pepper and serve.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups whole-milk Greek yogurt
  • 1 ripe Fuyu persimmon, sliced
  • 1/2 cup pomegranate arils
  • 1/3 cup hazelnuts, roughly chopped
  • 2 Tbsp hemp hearts
  • 1 tsp orange zest
  • 2 tsp raw honey
  • Pinch of cinnamon
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toast the hazelnuts in a dry skillet over medium heat 3 to 4 minutes until fragrant.
  • Divide the yogurt between 2 bowls.
  • Top with the persimmon slices and pomegranate arils.
  • Sprinkle with the hazelnuts, hemp hearts, orange zest, and cinnamon.
  • Drizzle with honey and serve.

Ingredients
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 bunch Swiss chard, stems diced and leaves chopped
  • 4 pasture-raised eggs
  • 1/3 cup feta, crumbled
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat and cook the onion, bell pepper, and chard stems 6 to 7 minutes until soft.
  • Add the garlic, cumin, and paprika and cook 1 minute.
  • Pour in the tomatoes, season with salt and pepper, and simmer 8 minutes.
  • Stir in the chard leaves and cook until wilted.
  • Make 4 wells and crack an egg into each, then cover and cook 6 to 8 minutes until the whites are set.
  • Top with feta and parsley and serve.

Ingredients
  • 2 ripe kiwis, peeled
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1/2 avocado
  • 3 Tbsp hemp hearts
  • 1 scoop unflavored collagen or protein powder
  • Juice of 1/2 lime
  • 1 1/4 cups unsweetened coconut water
  • 1/2 cup ice
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Add the coconut water and spinach to a blender and blend until smooth.
  • Add the kiwis, banana, avocado, hemp hearts, protein, and lime juice.
  • Blend until creamy, adding ice for a thicker texture.
  • Pour into 2 glasses and serve immediately.

Ingredients
  • 4 slices seeded rye bread
  • 6 oz smoked mackerel, skin removed and flaked
  • 1/2 cup whole-milk Greek yogurt
  • 1 Tbsp fresh dill, chopped
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • 1/2 small red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 small cucumber, thinly sliced
  • Black pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toss the red onion with the vinegar and a pinch of salt and let sit 10 minutes to quick-pickle.
  • Mix the yogurt with the dill, lemon zest, and black pepper.
  • Toast the rye bread.
  • Spread the yogurt on each slice and top with cucumber and flaked mackerel.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup teff grain
  • 3 cups water
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 6 fresh or dried figs, quartered
  • 1/2 cup walnuts, chopped
  • 1 tsp orange zest
  • 2 tsp maple syrup
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toast the teff in a dry saucepan over medium heat 2 minutes, stirring constantly.
  • Add the water and salt, bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer.
  • Cook 15 to 18 minutes, stirring often, until thick and creamy.
  • Stir in the almond milk, cinnamon, and vanilla and cook 2 more minutes.
  • Divide between bowls and top with figs, walnuts, orange zest, and maple syrup.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup steel-cut oats
  • 3 1/2 cups low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 4 cups chopped kale or chard
  • 4 pasture-raised eggs
  • 1/3 cup grated Parmesan
  • 1 Tbsp butter
  • Red pepper flakes
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Bring the broth to a boil, stir in the oats, and simmer 22 to 25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until tender.
  • Meanwhile, heat the olive oil in a skillet and cook the garlic 1 minute, then add the greens and cook until wilted.
  • Season the greens with salt and red pepper flakes and set aside.
  • Fry the eggs in the butter to your liking.
  • Stir the Parmesan into the oats and season with salt and pepper.
  • Top each bowl with greens and a fried egg.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb wild shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 2 cups cooked short-grain brown rice
  • 2 sheets nori, cut into thin strips
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 cup shelled edamame, steamed

  • Whisk the coconut aminos, sesame oil, and rice vinegar in a small bowl.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups cooked chicken breast, shredded
  • 2 cups red seedless grapes
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 bunch lacinato kale, stems removed and thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup pecans, toasted
  • 1/3 cup goat cheese, crumbled
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp honey
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the oven to 425F and roast the grapes with 1 Tbsp olive oil and a pinch of salt 15 minutes until blistered.
  • Whisk the 3 Tbsp olive oil, balsamic, Dijon, honey, salt, and pepper for the dressing.
  • Massage the kale with half the dressing for 1 minute until softened.
  • Toss in the chicken, roasted grapes, and pecans.
  • Top with goat cheese and the remaining dressing.

Ingredients
  • 1 Tbsp coconut oil
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and diced
  • 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 4 cups low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 1/3 cup natural peanut butter
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne
  • 1 bunch collard greens, stems removed and chopped
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 1/4 cup roasted peanuts, chopped
  • Salt to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the coconut oil in a large pot and cook the onion 5 minutes.
  • Add the garlic, ginger, cumin, and cayenne and cook 1 minute.
  • Add the sweet potatoes, tomatoes, and broth, bring to a boil, then simmer 15 minutes.
  • Whisk the peanut butter with a ladle of hot broth and stir it into the pot.
  • Add the collards and simmer 8 minutes until tender.
  • Finish with lime juice and salt and top with chopped peanuts.

  • 3/4 cup quick-cooking farro
  • 2 cups cooked chicken, shredded

Ingredients
  • 2 cans (6 oz each) wild salmon with bones, drained
  • 1 pasture-raised egg
  • 1/3 cup almond flour
  • 2 scallions, minced
  • 1 Tbsp fresh dill, chopped
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 5 cups arugula
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 Tbsp capers, chopped
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Mash the salmon with the egg, almond flour, scallions, dill, Dijon, salt, and pepper.
  • Form into 8 small patties.
  • Heat 2 Tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium heat and cook the patties 4 minutes per side until golden.
  • Whisk the 3 Tbsp olive oil, lemon juice, and capers for the dressing.
  • Toss the arugula and tomatoes with the dressing and top with the salmon cakes.

Ingredients
  • 4 slices uncured bacon, chopped
  • 12 oz mixed mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 6 cups baby spinach
  • 4 pasture-raised eggs
  • 1 Tbsp white vinegar
  • 2 Tbsp sherry or red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1/4 cup shaved Parmesan
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Cook the bacon in a large skillet until crisp, then remove and leave the fat in the pan.
  • Add the mushrooms and cook 6 to 8 minutes until browned, then add the garlic for 1 minute.
  • Whisk the sherry vinegar, Dijon, and olive oil and stir into the mushrooms to make a warm dressing.
  • Bring a pot of water with the white vinegar to a gentle simmer and poach the eggs 3 to 4 minutes.
  • Toss the spinach with the warm mushroom dressing until just wilted.
  • Top with bacon, a poached egg, and shaved Parmesan.

Ingredients
  • 2 lb fresh mussels, scrubbed and debearded
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 shallot, minced
  • 4 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup dry white wine or extra broth
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • 4 thick slices sourdough
  • 1 Tbsp butter
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the olive oil in a large pot and cook the shallot, garlic, and pepper flakes 2 minutes.
  • Add the tomatoes, broth, and wine and simmer 5 minutes.
  • Add the mussels, cover, and cook 5 to 7 minutes until they open. Discard any that stay closed.
  • Meanwhile, brush the sourdough with butter and grill or toast until charred at the edges.
  • Stir the parsley into the broth, season, and serve the mussels in bowls with the bread.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb grass-fed ground beef
  • 4 oz grass-fed beef liver, finely minced or pulsed in a food processor
  • 1 pasture-raised egg
  • 1/4 cup almond flour
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 lb rainbow carrots, halved lengthwise
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 cup whole-milk Greek yogurt
  • 1/4 cup fresh mint and parsley, chopped
  • Juice of 1/2 lemon
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the oven to 425F and toss the carrots with olive oil, salt, and pepper on a sheet pan.
  • Mix the beef, liver, egg, almond flour, cumin, paprika, garlic, salt, and pepper and roll into 16 meatballs.
  • Add the meatballs to the sheet pan with the carrots and roast 20 to 22 minutes until cooked through.
  • Stir the yogurt with the herbs, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt.
  • Serve the meatballs and carrots over the herb yogurt.

Ingredients
  • 8 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 2 Tbsp za'atar
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 3 medium beets, peeled and cut into wedges
  • 1 bunch Swiss chard or beet greens, chopped
  • 3 Tbsp tahini
  • 2 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 clove garlic, grated
  • 2 to 3 Tbsp warm water
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the oven to 425F and toss the beets with 1 Tbsp olive oil and salt on a sheet pan, then roast 10 minutes.
  • Rub the chicken with za'atar, 1 Tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper and add to the pan with the beets.
  • Roast 20 to 22 minutes until the chicken reaches 165F.
  • Whisk the tahini, lemon juice, garlic, water, and salt into a smooth sauce.
  • Saute the greens in the remaining olive oil until wilted and toss with half the tahini sauce.
  • Serve the chicken and beets over the greens with the remaining sauce.

Ingredients
  • 4 wild salmon fillets (6 oz each)
  • 1/2 cup walnuts, finely chopped
  • 2 Tbsp whole-grain mustard
  • 1 Tbsp raw honey
  • 1 Tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 large head broccoli, cut into florets
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, half sliced and half cut into wedges
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the oven to 425F.
  • Toss the broccoli with olive oil, salt, and pepper on a sheet pan and roast 10 minutes.
  • Mix the mustard and honey and spread over the salmon, then press the walnuts and parsley on top.
  • Add the salmon and lemon slices to the sheet pan and roast 12 minutes until the fish flakes.
  • Serve with lemon wedges.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/4 lb pork tenderloin
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1/2 small green cabbage, thinly sliced
  • 2 crisp apples, sliced
  • 1 yellow onion, sliced
  • 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 Tbsp whole-grain mustard
  • 1 Tbsp butter
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Season the pork with thyme, salt, and pepper and sear in 1 Tbsp olive oil in an oven-safe skillet 2 minutes per side.
  • Transfer the skillet to a 400F oven and roast 15 to 18 minutes until 145F inside, then rest on a board.
  • Meanwhile, cook the onion, cabbage, and apples in the remaining oil in another skillet 10 minutes until softened.
  • Add the broth, vinegar, and mustard to the pork skillet and simmer 2 minutes, then swirl in the butter.
  • Slice the pork and serve over the cabbage and apples with the pan sauce.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 cups red lentils, rinsed
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 Tbsp coconut oil
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 1 Tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 5 oz baby spinach
  • 1 head cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1/4 cup cilantro, chopped
  • Salt to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the oven to 425F and roast the cauliflower with olive oil, salt, and a pinch of curry powder 22 to 25 minutes.
  • Simmer the lentils with the water and turmeric 15 minutes until soft.
  • Meanwhile, heat the coconut oil and cook the onion 5 minutes, then add the ginger, garlic, curry powder, and cumin seeds for 1 minute.
  • Stir the onion mixture and tomatoes into the lentils and simmer 5 minutes.
  • Fold in the spinach until wilted and season with lemon juice and salt.
  • Serve the dal topped with roasted cauliflower and cilantro.
