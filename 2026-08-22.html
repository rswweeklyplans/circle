Focus: Protein Prioritization

This week's meals are built around Protein Prioritization. Each plate is designed to be whole-food, home-cook-friendly, and easy to prepare in 40 minutes or less.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup whole-milk cottage cheese
  • 2 pasture-raised eggs
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 cucumber, diced
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp everything seasoning
  • Sea salt and black pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Bring a small pot of water to a gentle boil and cook eggs 7 minutes for jammy yolks.
  • Transfer eggs to cold water, peel, and halve.
  • Spoon cottage cheese into a wide bowl and top with tomatoes and cucumber.
  • Add the eggs, drizzle with olive oil, and finish with seasoning, salt, and pepper.

Ingredients
  • 6 oz ground turkey
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 1/2 small onion, diced
  • 1 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 1/2 tsp fennel seed, crushed
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat avocado oil in a skillet over medium and cook onion 3 minutes.
  • Add turkey with fennel and paprika, breaking it up until browned, about 6 minutes.
  • Stir in spinach and cook until just wilted.
  • Whisk eggs with salt and pepper, pour into the pan, and fold gently until set.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 3 eggs
  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1 scoop vanilla protein powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 Tbsp butter for the pan
  • 1/2 cup fresh berries
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Blend oats, yogurt, eggs, protein powder, baking powder, and vanilla until smooth.
  • Let the batter rest 5 minutes to thicken.
  • Melt butter in a nonstick skillet over medium-low heat.
  • Cook 1/4-cup pancakes about 2 minutes per side until golden.
  • Serve topped with fresh berries.

Ingredients
  • 2 slices sprouted-grain bread
  • 4 oz smoked salmon
  • 1/2 cup whole-milk ricotta
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 Tbsp fresh dill, chopped
  • 1 Tbsp capers, drained
  • Black pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whip ricotta with lemon juice and a pinch of pepper until light.
  • Toast the bread until crisp.
  • Spread ricotta thickly over each slice.
  • Drape smoked salmon on top and finish with dill and capers.

Ingredients
  • 8 oz sirloin steak
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup cooked quinoa
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 Tbsp ghee
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Hot sauce to serve
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Season the steak generously with salt, pepper, and garlic powder and rest 10 minutes.
  • Sear in a hot skillet with ghee, 3 to 4 minutes per side, then rest and slice.
  • Fry the eggs in the same pan to your liking.
  • Build bowls with quinoa, sliced steak, eggs, and avocado.
  • Finish with hot sauce.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 scoop chocolate protein powder
  • 1 Tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 Tbsp almond butter
  • 1/2 frozen banana
  • 1 handful baby spinach
  • 1/2 cup ice
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Add almond milk and yogurt to the blender first.
  • Add protein powder, chia, almond butter, banana, and spinach.
  • Blend on high 45 seconds, adding ice until thick.
  • Pour into a tall glass and drink within 10 minutes.

Ingredients
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 oz nitrate-free ham, diced
  • 1/2 cup shredded Gruyere
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup diced red bell pepper
  • 1 Tbsp chives, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 375F and grease a 12-cup muffin tin.
  • Whisk eggs with cream, salt, and pepper.
  • Divide ham, pepper, and Gruyere among the cups.
  • Pour egg mixture over, filling each cup three-quarters full.
  • Bake 18 to 20 minutes until puffed and set, then top with chives.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb boneless chicken thighs
  • 1 cup cooked farro
  • 2 cups arugula
  • 1/2 cup roasted red peppers, sliced
  • 1/4 cup crumbled feta
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toss chicken with 1 Tbsp olive oil, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  • Grill or pan-sear over medium-high 5 to 6 minutes per side, then slice.
  • Whisk remaining oil with vinegar for a quick dressing.
  • Layer farro, arugula, peppers, and chicken in bowls.
  • Drizzle with dressing and scatter feta on top.

Ingredients
  • 2 ahi tuna steaks (6 oz each)
  • 1 can cannellini beans, rinsed
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 Tbsp parsley, chopped
  • Salt and cracked pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Pat tuna dry and season heavily with salt and cracked pepper.
  • Sear in a very hot oiled skillet 90 seconds per side, then slice thin.
  • Toss beans, onion, tomatoes, parsley, olive oil, and lemon juice.
  • Divide the bean salad between plates and fan the tuna over the top.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb ground turkey
  • 1 egg
  • 1/3 cup almond flour
  • 6 cups chicken bone broth
  • 2 zucchini, sliced into half moons
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Mix turkey with egg, almond flour, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper.
  • Roll into 1-inch meatballs.
  • Simmer broth with garlic and carrots for 5 minutes.
  • Drop in meatballs and simmer 10 minutes until cooked through.
  • Add zucchini and cook 3 more minutes, then season to taste.

Ingredients
  • 14 oz extra-firm tofu, pressed and cubed
  • 1 cup shelled edamame
  • 1 cup cooked brown rice
  • 2 Tbsp arrowroot starch
  • 2 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 3 Tbsp tamari
  • 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 2 scallions, sliced
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Toss tofu cubes with arrowroot until lightly coated.
  • Pan-fry in avocado oil over medium-high until crisp on all sides, about 10 minutes.
  • Warm the edamame and stir together tamari, vinegar, and sesame oil.
  • Build bowls with rice, tofu, and edamame.
  • Spoon the sauce over and finish with scallions.

  • 1/4 cup Greek yogurt
  • 1 Tbsp prepared horseradish

  • 1 cup shredded carrot

Ingredients
  • 1 lb shrimp, peeled and chopped
  • 3 bell peppers, halved and seeded
  • 1 cup cottage cheese
  • 1/2 cup cooked wild rice
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 Tbsp grated Parmesan
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 400F.
  • Saute garlic in olive oil, add shrimp and paprika, and cook 3 minutes.
  • Stir the shrimp into the cottage cheese and wild rice.
  • Fill the pepper halves and top with Parmesan.
  • Bake 20 minutes until the peppers soften and the tops brown.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb cooked shredded chicken
  • 1 1/2 cups cooked green lentils
  • 1 cup diced cucumber
  • 1/2 cup chopped mint
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 3 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, cumin, salt, and pepper in the bottom of four jars.
  • Layer lentils over the dressing so they marinate.
  • Add cucumber, then the shredded chicken.
  • Top with mint, seal, and refrigerate up to four days.
  • Shake well before eating.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 lb pork tenderloin
  • 1 lb green beans, trimmed
  • 3 Tbsp butter
  • 4 cloves garlic, smashed
  • 2 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 1 Tbsp avocado oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 400F and season the pork all over with salt and pepper.
  • Sear in an oven-safe skillet with avocado oil until browned on all sides, about 6 minutes.
  • Add butter, garlic, and thyme and baste the pork for 1 minute.
  • Scatter green beans around the pork and roast 15 to 18 minutes to 145F.
  • Rest 5 minutes before slicing.

Ingredients
  • 4 cod fillets (6 oz each)
  • 3 Tbsp white miso paste
  • 1 Tbsp honey
  • 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 lb baby bok choy, halved
  • 1 Tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 Tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk miso, honey, and vinegar into a glaze.
  • Heat oven to 425F and line a sheet pan with parchment.
  • Brush cod generously with the glaze and arrange on the pan.
  • Toss bok choy with sesame oil and ginger and add to the pan.
  • Roast 12 to 14 minutes until the fish flakes, then top with sesame seeds.

Ingredients
  • 8 bone-in chicken thighs
  • 1 can chickpeas, rinsed
  • 1/2 cup Castelvetrano olives
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 1/2 cups chicken broth
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Season the thighs with salt, pepper, and paprika.
  • Brown skin-side down in olive oil over medium-high, about 7 minutes, then flip and remove.
  • Soften the onion in the drippings for 4 minutes.
  • Return the chicken and add broth, chickpeas, olives, and lemon.
  • Simmer covered 25 minutes, then uncover 5 minutes to crisp the skin.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/4 lb ground bison
  • 2 sweet potatoes, diced small
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 2 cups chopped kale
  • 2 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat avocado oil in a large skillet and cook sweet potatoes covered for 10 minutes, stirring twice.
  • Add onion and cook 3 minutes more.
  • Push vegetables aside, add bison, and brown well, about 7 minutes.
  • Stir in cumin, chili powder, salt, and pepper.
  • Fold in kale and cook 2 minutes until just wilted.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 lb large shrimp, peeled
  • 1 large spaghetti squash, halved
  • 4 Tbsp butter
  • 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup dry white wine
  • 2 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/4 cup parsley, chopped
  • Red pepper flakes to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Roast the squash cut-side down at 400F for 35 minutes, then shred with a fork.
  • Melt butter in a skillet and cook garlic 1 minute until fragrant.
  • Add shrimp and cook 2 minutes per side until pink.
  • Pour in wine and lemon juice and simmer 2 minutes.
  • Toss with squash strands, parsley, and red pepper flakes.

Ingredients
  • 4 salmon fillets (6 oz each)
  • 1 1/2 cups cooked French lentils
  • 1/4 cup Dijon mustard
  • 1/2 cup almond flour
  • 2 Tbsp fresh dill, chopped
  • 2 Tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat oven to 400F and line a baking sheet.
  • Combine almond flour, dill, parsley, and olive oil into a crumb.

Ingredients
  • 2 1/2 lb beef chuck roast
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1 cup whole-milk ricotta
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Season the chuck roast well and sear all sides in a hot skillet.
  • Transfer to the slow cooker with tomatoes, onion, garlic, broth, and Italian seasoning.
  • Cook on low 8 hours until the beef shreds easily.
  • Shred with two forks and stir back into the sauce.
  • Serve in bowls with a generous spoonful of ricotta.

Ingredients
