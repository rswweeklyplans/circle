Focus: Metabolic Flexibility

This week's meals are built around Metabolic Flexibility. Each plate is designed to be whole-food, home-cook-friendly, and easy to prepare in 40 minutes or less.

Ingredients
  • 8 oz grass-fed sirloin or flank steak
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced into strips
  • 1/2 red onion, sliced
  • 2 Tbsp avocado oil, divided
  • 1 Tbsp butter or ghee
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Season the steak with salt, pepper, and smoked paprika.
  • Sear in 1 Tbsp avocado oil over high heat for 3 to 4 minutes per side, then rest 5 minutes.
  • Cook the peppers and onion in the same pan with the remaining oil until charred at the edges, about 6 minutes.
  • Fry the eggs in butter to your liking.
  • Slice the steak against the grain and serve with the peppers and eggs.

Ingredients
  • 8 large eggs
  • 2 cups baby spinach, chopped
  • 1/2 cup crumbled feta
  • 1/4 cup sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil for the pan
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/4 tsp sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the oven to 375F and oil 8 cups of a muffin tin.
  • Whisk the eggs with the oregano and salt.
  • Divide the spinach, feta, sun-dried tomatoes, and scallions among the cups.
  • Pour the egg over the fillings.
  • Bake 18 to 20 minutes until set and lightly golden.
  • Store extras in the fridge for up to four days.

Ingredients
  • 1/4 cup almond flour
  • 2 Tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 2 Tbsp chia seeds
  • 2 Tbsp hemp hearts
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 Tbsp almond butter
  • 1/4 cup raspberries
  • Pinch of sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk the almond flour, flax, chia, hemp hearts, cinnamon, and salt in a small saucepan.
  • Stir in the almond milk and vanilla.
  • Simmer over medium-low heat for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring, until thick.
  • Spoon into a bowl and top with almond butter and raspberries.

Ingredients
  • 1/2 cup pumpkin puree

  • 2 Tbsp collagen peptides or unflavored protein powder
  • 1 Tbsp almond butter
  • 1 tsp pumpkin pie spice

Prep Notes / Recipe

Ingredients
  • 4 fully cooked chicken sausages (about 12 oz), sliced
  • 4 cups riced cauliflower
  • 1 can (4 oz) diced green chiles
  • 1/2 yellow onion, diced
  • 2 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 2 Tbsp chopped cilantro
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Brown the sausage in 1 Tbsp oil over medium-high heat for 4 minutes, then set aside.
  • Cook the onion in the remaining oil until soft, about 3 minutes.
  • Add the riced cauliflower, cumin, salt, and pepper and cook 6 to 8 minutes until golden in spots.
  • Stir in the green chiles and the sausage and heat through.
  • Top with avocado and cilantro.

Ingredients
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 cup cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 1/4 cup shredded Gruyere
  • 1 Tbsp butter, divided
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 cup arugula
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Cook the mushrooms and thyme in half the butter until browned, about 5 minutes, then set aside.
  • Whisk the eggs with a pinch of salt and pour into the pan with the remaining butter over medium-low heat.
  • When the eggs are nearly set, add the mushrooms and Gruyere to one side and fold.
  • Toss the arugula with the olive oil and lemon juice.
  • Serve the omelet with the arugula on the side.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs

  • 3 Tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 2 Tbsp lemon juice

  • 1 tsp dried oregano

Prep Notes / Recipe

Ingredients
  • 6 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
  • 3 Tbsp avocado oil mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 2 celery stalks, finely diced
  • 2 Tbsp chopped cilantro
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 4 large collard green leaves
  • 1 carrot, shredded
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Stir the mayonnaise, curry powder, and lemon juice together in a bowl.
  • Fold in the eggs, celery, and cilantro and season with salt and pepper.
  • Shave down the thick stem on each collard leaf so it rolls easily.
  • Divide the egg salad and shredded carrot among the leaves.
  • Fold in the sides and roll up like a burrito.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb ground turkey
  • 1 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 2 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 6 cups chopped romaine
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 cup sliced radishes
  • 1 avocado
  • 1/4 cup sour cream or Greek yogurt
  • 2 Tbsp lime juice
  • 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Brown the turkey in the avocado oil over medium-high heat for 6 to 8 minutes.
  • Stir in the chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, and salt with 2 Tbsp water and cook 2 more minutes.
  • Blend or mash the avocado with the sour cream, lime juice, and a pinch of salt until smooth.
  • Divide the romaine, tomatoes, and radishes among bowls.
  • Top with the turkey, a spoonful of crema, and cilantro.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 8 oz green beans, trimmed
  • 4 hard-boiled eggs, quartered
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 Tbsp whole-grain mustard
  • 1 small shallot, minced
  • 2 Tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Boil the potatoes in salted water for 12 minutes until just tender.
  • Add the green beans for the last 3 minutes, then drain everything.
  • Spread on a plate and chill in the fridge for at least 20 minutes. Cooling the potatoes builds resistant starch.
  • Whisk the olive oil, vinegar, mustard, and shallot.
  • Toss the cooled potatoes and beans with the dressing and parsley, then top with the eggs.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 4 medium zucchini, spiralized
  • 2 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 2 Tbsp avocado oil, divided
  • 3 Tbsp almond butter
  • 2 Tbsp coconut aminos
  • 1 Tbsp fish sauce
  • 2 Tbsp lime juice
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup bean sprouts
  • 3 scallions, sliced
  • 1/4 cup chopped roasted peanuts or cashews
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Whisk the almond butter, coconut aminos, fish sauce, and lime juice with 2 Tbsp warm water.
  • Sear the shrimp in 1 Tbsp oil for 2 minutes per side, then remove.
  • Scramble the eggs in the same pan and push to one side.
  • Add the remaining oil, garlic, and zucchini noodles and toss for 2 minutes so they stay crisp.
  • Return the shrimp, pour in the sauce, and toss with the bean sprouts.
  • Top with scallions and chopped nuts.

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 3 Tbsp coconut aminos
  • 1 Tbsp rice vinegar

Ingredients
  • 4 wild salmon fillets (6 oz each)
  • 1/3 cup basil pesto
  • 3 medium zucchini, cut into half moons
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the oven to 425F.
  • Toss the zucchini, tomatoes, and garlic with the olive oil, salt, and pepper on a sheet pan and roast 10 minutes.
  • Push the vegetables aside and add the salmon to the pan.
  • Spread the pesto over each fillet.
  • Roast 10 to 12 minutes until the salmon flakes easily.
  • Serve with lemon wedges.

Ingredients
  • 8 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  • 2 delicata squash, seeded and sliced into half moons
  • 1 red onion, cut into wedges
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 10 fresh sage leaves
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp sea salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the oven to 425F.
  • Pat the chicken dry and season with the garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  • Toss the squash, onion, and sage with the olive oil on a large sheet pan.
  • Nestle the chicken skin-side up among the vegetables.
  • Roast 35 minutes until the skin is crisp and the chicken reaches 175F.
  • Splash the vegetables with the vinegar before serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 lb Italian sausage links
  • 2 bunches broccolini, trimmed
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 red onion, cut into wedges
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 Tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Prep Notes / Recipe
  • Heat the oven to 425F.
  • Toss the peppers and onion with half the olive oil, the oregano, salt, and pepper on a sheet pan.
  • Add the sausages and roast 15 minutes.
  • Toss the broccolini with the remaining oil and red pepper flakes and add it to the pan.
  • Roast 12 to 15 more minutes until the sausages are browned and cooked through.
  • Drizzle with the balsamic vinegar before serving.

  • 1/3 cup coconut aminos
  • 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 Tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 Tbsp avocado oil, divided
