RSW — Power Split

Program: Power Split — 8-Week Upper/Lower Strength Split • Week 2 of 8
Goal of Week 2: Lock In Your Weights: Repeat the movements and confirm your working weights. Still 2–3 reps in reserve — technique first, load second.

Week 2 is about consistency. Repeat the movements, dial in your form, and confirm your working weights. You should feel more confident under the heavier loads than last week.

This phase: compounds 6–8 reps • accessories 8–10 • isolation 12–15. When all sets hit the top of the rep range with good form, add 2.5–10 lb. Training at home? Bump the reps up (compounds 8–12, accessories 10–15) and lower for 3 slow seconds to make lighter weights feel heavy.

Weekly Cardio & Steps

Get in 2–4 Zone 2 walks this week (30–45 minutes at a pace where you can still hold a conversation), plus one optional 10–15 minute interval session. Aim for 8,000–10,000 daily steps — walking supports recovery, regulates hormones, and keeps your metabolism active between sessions.
